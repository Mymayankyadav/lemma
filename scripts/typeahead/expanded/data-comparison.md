# Training-data comparison and exclusions

The archived 500,002,914-token corpus is unchanged. Its verified manifest identifies 613,502 training documents and 300,000,971 / 150,001,934 / 50,000,009 explanation / proof / public-Q&A tokens. Q&A answers are public forum writing, not verified learner writing. The locally archived corpus contains previously extracted text; restoring original MathML/HTML requires its original source files, not this archive.

`data_audit.py` preserves plain text and extracts conservative HTML/MathML with a source-codepoint span per emitted codepoint; `utf16_spans` translates these spans for browser offsets. MathML without an alternative is rejected. Formula spans are masked while HTML is parsed. This is not a general-purpose HTML or notation repair system. Search normalization is separate from display. Connected groups use source families, exact surface text, complete short-passage containment, and high-overlap shingles with case/notation and polarity guards, using a bounded bottom-four candidate search. These guards deliberately trade recall for fewer false merges. The similarity screen can still miss paraphrases and make false merges: human review is needed before corpus decisions.

`sealed_data_check.py` is a *custodian-side* bounded checker that emits only aggregate counts. It compares whole-document hashes, shared 12-token sequences and high-overlap shingles. It is not encryption, does not itself enforce access control, and is not proof of absence of leakage. Its substring signal is conservative and requires review. Run it separately in a trusted environment with the custodian's reserved text; never copy that text or index into this repository. No reserved text was read in the present work, and no sealed check has been performed.

`gsm_train_audit.py` checks only the pinned official GSM8K **train** JSONL against every archived training-document content hash; it strips calculator annotations and the final answer line from rationale text. The source is arithmetic rather than proof or notation prose. It is MIT licensed, copyright 2021 OpenAI, revision `3101c7d5072418e28b9008a6636bde82a006892c`, URL https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math/tree/3101c7d5072418e28b9008a6636bde82a006892c. The local check covers exact whole-document matches only; substrings, paraphrases and sealed exclusions remain unverified. Its 337,676 rationale tokens do not satisfy the proposed 2–5M-token source gate. Do not treat this source as an approved new-material experiment.

`kn_matched_data.py` prepares a bounded sample from train shards only. It links duplicate/source families before assigning a group-held diagnostic partition, holds this diagnostic stream fixed, makes D0 use original text at 60/30/10, and makes D1/D2 use cleaned and duplicate-link-deduplicated text within each register at 60/30/10 and 40/20/40 respectively. Publisher-family or short-passage containment alone is not grounds to discard a document; only exact and high-overlap revision links trigger filtering. Cross-register duplicate-linked documents can both remain in an arm; all linked documents are assigned together before the diagnostic split. It selects and clips N233 sentence-token streams at exact per-register quotas, preserving sentence boundaries and using the existing Kneser–Ney builder and serialized v2 format. All three arms share the D0 vocabulary, order 4 and 20 MiB ceiling. The diagnostic is drawn from the archived *training* pool; the packer's `validation` field is therefore not an independent validation claim. Token-model perplexity is not edit accuracy, action cost, an on-device result, or evidence of learner benefit. Run each arm in the same process using scratch space; the command fails on source or quota shortfalls:

```sh
python scripts/typeahead/expanded/kn_matched_data.py \
  --corpus /path/to/pinned-full-corpus --out /path/to/new-scratch-directory \
  --max-docs 4500 --tokens 500000
```

The sample is a deterministic prefix of the archive, not a corpus-wide probability sample. The small Tutor contrast families are synthetic implementation fixtures. They are not learner traces, commissioned cases, independent labels, or an 800-case benchmark.

`excerpt_audit.py` selects a reproducible 400-excerpt tranche: 228 explanation, 41 proof and 131 public-Q&A excerpts in the current 3,000-document training prefix. It stratifies source, register, length and formula presence; the oversampled selection is not a corpus-wide prevalence estimate. The receipt records only strata and an ID digest; the optional selection file contains IDs for a future reviewer and stays outside the repository. Two independent reviewers must still assess the plan's register, validity, boilerplate, attribution, notation and template questions; report agreement, disagreements and weighted intervals only after actual review.

The commissioned Tutor scenario benchmark is also outstanding: 0 of the proposed 800 human-authored scenarios exist here. A future commissioning schema should record a scenario/family ID and author/template family, visible question, learner-draft context, scope (subject, step and mode), candidate actions and allowed/withheld expectation, plus a neutral control and directional/no-hint/conflict variants where applicable. Keep every variant and paraphrase in its family before partitioning; allocate 400 fit, 200 calibration and 200 independent challenge cases. A blinded mathematical reviewer must review the authored cases and safety/withholding judgments. These cases are separate from reserved public threads; the four synthetic families in `fixtures/tutor_contrasts.json` cannot fill that allocation.

An assistant’s manual, nonrepresentative check of three same-publisher pairs found unrelated mathematical problems or pages, including different calculus and precalculus exercises. They remain linked for partitioning, but publisher membership alone no longer removes them from a training arm. The pair-review receipt records counts and an ID digest without copying the excerpts. Purpose-built copied, near-revision, opposite-polarity and notation-case fixtures exercise detector behavior; they are not an estimate of real false-merge or miss rates. A deliberately short, mathematically equivalent derivative paraphrase is a known missed pair in the fixture tests. The bounded minimum-hash lookup still misses some revisions, and the sealed and full-corpus substring checks are outstanding.

The completed 500,000-token-per-arm experiment and stream-reuse verification are recorded in `evidence/matched-kn-500k-training-diagnostic.json`. The same D0-fitted vocabulary, 4-gram order and 20 MiB ceiling were used; actual serialized sizes were about 10 MiB. D0 and D1 were retained only after their corrected training streams, the shared diagnostic stream and vocabulary regenerated byte-for-byte; D2 was rebuilt after correcting TeX-environment extraction. The 50,000-token grouped training diagnostic has 45,213 scored targets. Perplexities of 615.72, 445.14 and 345.86 for D0, D1 and D2 describe that sample only, not user outcomes.

A separate count-only check used the predeclared public development slice, 12 threads and 350 frozen events; `evidence/matched-kn-500k-public-dev.json` contains hashes, denominators, exact hits, and paired gains and losses. Restricting to 259 lowercase ASCII word-plus-space targets gives 162 boundary and 97 prefix opportunities. D0 recovered 42 and 57 in its top three, D1 recovered 40 and 57, D2 recovered 41 and 59, and the shipping 500M general pack recovered 52 and 75. D2 versus D1 gained five and lost four boundary hits, and gained five and lost three prefix hits. This small diagnostic does not establish a product lift or support replacing the shipping pack. It scores only the general count asset: subject packs, touch decoding, visible menus, policy and action costs are excluded. It is not an independent validation, and the slice has not passed the new sealed duplicate screen.

After building the packs, reproduce the fixed-event check with the same public development directory and model directories:

```sh
python scripts/typeahead/expanded/compare_kn_events.py \
  --split /path/to/public-development-slice \
  --model D0 /path/to/D0-model --model D1 /path/to/D1-model \
  --model D2 /path/to/D2-model --model shipping public/models/typeahead/ngram-v2 \
  --out /path/to/new-summary.json
```
