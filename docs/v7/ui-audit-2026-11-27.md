**What was not done.**
- Chromium is not WebKit: iPadOS status-bar blur, `100vh`, backdrop filters, the native keyboard's real geometry, Pencil and haptics need a device.
- Light mode, by request.
- Hover, press and focus were probed on an iPad with a pointer, not on every size; touch devices were not probed for sticky hover.
- Contrast is computed over flat colours; gradients and images are not sampled.
- The motion run used one device at a time but not an isolated machine; frame timings are indicative (§4).
- The `core` set (one scene per route plus the drawers) was used for the ten added device sizes, with the deep-link, loading and first-use / stress scenes on top; only the five original sizes (`phone-s`, `phone-p`, `phone-l`, `ipad-p`, `ipad-l`) have the full catalogue, and only they have whole-page frames in the archive.
- The images in the archive are 1× lossy WebP (the 2× PNG originals are about 760 MB and are not committed); measurements were taken at 2× before conversion.
- The sweep ran on 11 pages per device (`phone-p`, `ipad-l`, `desktop`), 8 controls per page, not on every page of every device.

## 3. Findings

### 3.1 The pink boundary (A)

**A1 · The heading focus ring.**
- `src/app/v7/useRouteReadingFocus.ts` gives the page's `h1` `tabIndex=-1` and focuses it after a route commit when nothing else has focus. `src/ui/v7/kit-system.css:30` styles `:is(button,input,select,textarea,a,summary,[tabindex]):focus-visible` with `outline: var(--v7-focus-width) solid var(--v7-role-focus-ring)` (`#D885A2`, 2 px as computed) and a `box-shadow` gap in the page colour.
- An `h1` is a block element, so the ring is as wide as the column: 358 px on a phone, 868 px beside the iPad rail (`today` on `ipad-l`).
- It shows on a cold launch, after browser back and after keyboard navigation, but **not** after tapping a link (focus stays on the link). `ringcheck.mjs`: cold load `H1 :focus-visible`, tap nav `A` without ring, keyboard nav `A` with ring, back `A` with ring.
- Measured: a focus-visible heading on 84 of 172 scenes (`phone-s`), 84 of 172 scenes (`phone-p`), 85 of 172 scenes (`phone-l`), 85 of 172 scenes (`ipad-p`), 87 of 172 scenes (`ipad-l`); 49–68 % of the scenes on every size.
- Suggested: mark the reading anchor (`data-reading-anchor`) and give it `outline: none`; keep the ring for controls. The preview in §5 does this with `h1[tabindex="-1"]:focus{outline:none}` and the pink-edge count on Today falls from 1 to 0.

**A2 · Field focus is a nested box.** Capture, the editors and Search focus their field on open: a 2 px pink outline (`#D885A2`) sits 2 px outside the field's own 1 px grey border (`#9C9A92`), so a text field reads as two nested boxes, one of them pink (`capture-empty`, `task-editor`, `new-path`, `card-new`; `.v7-capture-field:focus` in `shell.css:158`). **A3 · Sheets focus the × button on open** (`card-new`, `calendar-quick-add`, `calendar-block-sheet`, `calendar-planner` on phone portrait), so a pink box surrounds the close icon before the person touches anything.

**A4 · Pink carries six jobs:** focus ring, primary action, link, selected chip/tab/segment, badge and the Calendar's dashed *+* slots (plus the heat map). A focus ring in the same colour as a selection cannot be told from it. 62–86 % of scenes per device contain at least one pink border or outline.

### 3.2 Dark mode: contrast and layering (B)

**Text is fine; surfaces are not.** Computed text contrast: the median of each scene is 8.3–12.6:1 across devices; 66 of 4,546 text elements are below AA on phone portrait (1.5 %), the largest group being B3 below.

**B1 · The ladder is compressed.** `src/ui/v7/tokens.css`, `graph-night`:

| Layer | Token | Value | Contrast with the layer below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunken | `surface-sunken` | `#1F1E1D` | — |
| Page | `surface-page` | `#262624` | 1.10 over sunken |
| Raised card, dock, sheet | `surface-raised` = `surface-overlay` = `surface-muted` | `#30302E` | **1.15** over page |
| Lifted (menus, toasts) | `surface-lifted` | `#393937` | 1.14 over raised |
| Card border | `border-subtle` | `#3D3D3A` | **1.21** over the card |

Median card-over-page contrast per scene is 1.15:1 across device sizes; the median card border is 1.21–1.53:1 (lowest on portrait phones; some cards use `border-default`). A card, the dock and a sheet all share one fill, so a card scrolling behind the dock fuses with it (`settings-tutor` on phone portrait).

**B2 · Grid and borders are one token.** The paper grid is `--v7-default-grid`, built from `--v7-role-border-subtle` (`src/ui/v7/kit-system.css:21`): the same `#3D3D3A` that outlines every card. Card edges therefore have exactly the contrast of the paper lines around them, which is much of the "washed" look and the "mismatching rails" feeling, and Calendar's hour lines add a second grid on top (C2).

**B5 · One screen already has the deeper ground.** The Atlas sky and Focus sit on `#1F1E1D` (sampled from `atlas-map.png`); every other screen sits on `#262624`. Atlas reads richer for that reason alone.

**B3 · Selected-state pink fails AA.** The selection foreground `#E3A0B8` on the selected fills: `#494947` 4.3:1 and `#515150` 3.77:1 (the two most frequent failures in the per-scene lists, in Paths filters, segmented controls and tabs); `#F17059` on `#393937` 3.97:1; `#939290` on `#30302E` 4.27:1.

**B6 · Chart marks are as loud as text.** The Progress *Focus by day* bars are filled `rgb(194,192,182)` = `#C2C0B6` (`viz-neutral`), the same value as secondary text and 7.25:1 on a card, so they are the brightest shapes on the screen.

### 3.3 Rails and layout (C)

**C1 · Left edges on Today (390 px).** `alignment.bySel` for `today`: `header`, hub row, `h1` and cards at x = 16; the dock row at 17; the tab bar at 21; ghost button 25; sub-heads and secondary buttons 29; the hero's title and primary button 41. Six left edges within 25 px. Scenes with at least one near-miss (1–8 px) pair of block edges: 139 of 172 (`phone-s`), 139 of 172 (`phone-p`), 65 of 172 (`phone-l`), 85 of 172 (`ipad-p`), 84 of 172 (`ipad-l`) (a screening signal; nesting is partly intended).

**C2 · Calendar Week** (`calendar-week`, phone and iPad portrait):
- the *1 overdue* chip and *Add all* touch the weekday letters;
- the two overlapping Monday events are laid side by side, each 91 px wide, at x = 136 and x = 231, while a day is 95 px wide (header centres 189 → 284): the second sits in Tuesday's column;
- the page's graph paper shows through the hour grid (two grids);
- on iPad portrait the Day / Week / Month / Agenda control is its own row above the date navigation, and in landscape the mini-month card takes the left pane, leaving the week about half of the width.

**C3 · Wide rail clipped.** At 1180 × 788 the Library list has six rows (Inbox, Cards, Atlas, Tasks, Practice, Time log); *Practice* starts at y = 722 and *Time log* at y = 770, while the pinned Settings row starts at y = 740 and the screen ends at 788. No element in the rail scrolls (`today` on `ipad-l`).

**C4 · Close the day.** At the end of the scroll the last content's bottom is below the footer bar's top by 49 px (on every size except `desktop` (no fixed footer there); `endClear`). The primary action is a right-aligned floating pill; every other primary action is a full-width bar.

**C5 · Tutor start on iPad portrait** uses about 300 px of a 1148 px page (the card), then nothing. **C6 · Path detail on iPad landscape** sets text lines 1,000–1,100 px long (`path-plan`), and the *Continue with tutor* bar is 1,116 px wide. **C7 · Settings on iPad landscape** truncates its index sublabels ("Checkpoint chat · Connector used" needs 217 px of 170).

### 3.4 Drawers and sheets (D)

- **D1 Inconsistent frames.** Capture has a left-aligned title and a ×; the editors have *Back*, a centred title ("TASK / New task") and ×. Sheet heights differ at every step (`capture-empty`, `capture-task-typed`, `task-editor`, `task-editor-more`).
- **D2 Stacked pickers.** Due, Path, Priority and Subject open a second sheet over the editor, which stays visible above it with its own radius and a second scrim (`task-editor-due`, `-priority`, `-subject`).
- **D3 Estimate control.** A lone pink stub at 0 with tick labels (0 / 120 / 180) beneath it, on a slider with a single mark and no track (`task-editor-more`, `progress-add-goal`).
- **D4 Silent type switch.** Typing "Read Rudin chapter 4 tomorrow 5pm" in a Task capture turns it into a Time block with two chips (`capture-task-typed`).
- **D5 The dock floats over content.** On a 390 × 844 phone the dock is 66 px tall and sits at y = 732–798, over whatever scrolls behind it; because it shares the cards' fill, a card or the next row's text appears to continue past it (`settings-tutor`, `inbox-plans`).

### 3.7 Interaction states (G)

`states.mjs` sampled one control per distinct type (element, role and v7 class) on 11 screens (today, paths, path-plan, calendar-day, tasks, review-hub, settings-appearance, cards, progress, capture-sheet, atlas) with a pointer and motion on, comparing the control's own box at rest, on hover (380 ms later) and while pressed (320 ms): background colour and image, text colour, border, shadow, transform, opacity, outline and filter. 49 distinct controls were sampled (the rail and tab bar repeat on every screen and are counted once).
