## Failures found and fixed

The first inspection found the following failures. All were fixed in #393, merged 30 Oct.

- **Paths disagreed with Today about delays.** Real Analysis showed "3 days behind" on Today but "17 days behind" on Paths, and Linear Algebra showed "8 days ahead" against "16 days behind". Paths now uses the same 28-day pace as Today.
- **Good looked like Again.** In both worlds Good used the accent red, which is almost the same as the Again red. Good now has its own colour, `--grade-good` (graph-paper blue).
- **Atlas lost the grid.** Atlas painted an opaque background over the graph paper, and the Demo chip covered its back button.
- **Demo targets were labelled with their own dates.** Labels read like "22 Jan: Jan 22, 2027". They now name the event instead (Final exam, Midterm, Qualifier).
- **Session chips on Review cut goals mid-word.**

## Connector gate

- `tools/list` went over its 15,000-character budget after MCP-4 (15,924). It was brought back under the limit by #389 and #390, which measured 14,479.
