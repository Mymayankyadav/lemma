## N214 Calendar views and block editing

Day and Week place suggestions alongside study and busy time. An individual ghost can be added from the grid and restored with Undo. The date pill and view switch sit in the header, and Browse day opens on demand. Header actions wrap on narrow screens and at large text sizes. Creation and edits share the time block sheet. The Month key opens in a sheet.
