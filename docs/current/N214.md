## N214 Calendar views and block editing

Day and Week place suggestions alongside study and busy time. An individual ghost can be added from the grid and restored with Undo. Calendar uses the shared hub title with no subtitle. Token-sized arrows move weeks, or months in Month view, with date clamping. The ellipsis contains Go to today, Browse day, Plan study tasks and Choose date in that order. Header actions wrap on narrow screens and at large text sizes. Creation and edits share the time block sheet. A task scheduling link carries the task, its title and the requested duration into the form. The 50-minute preview stays visible above the form when it opens on a phone. The Month key opens in a sheet.

Plan study tasks retains ranked study times, kept counts and Add/Undo from every Calendar view. Week suggestions use a centered plus that fits short slots and keeps the full accessible action label. Phone Agenda retains overdue triage, and Reschedule opens the correct task without overlapping sheets.
