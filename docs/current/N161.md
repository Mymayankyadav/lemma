## N161 — phone layout fixes: Today's Continue cards, the Atlas concept sheet, the Calendar ruler

**Owner task** (13 Nov 2026, three phone screenshots): "The bottom bar is not aligned. And in second buttons are not visible" … "This one just comes in a way maybe it could have better if it was part of the dock in some way." … "In peek hide those buttons or lower the dock further so they are not visible as they make it look broken".

- **Today › Continue.** The tile was a grid with `align-content:start`, so the meter's `margin-top:auto` had no spare space: a card whose subtitle wrapped put its meter lower than its neighbour's. The tile is now four rows (cover, title, subtitle taking the spare space, meter): every tile ends on one line and every meter sits on one line.
- **Atlas › concept sheet.** The peek was clamped to 180 px with `overflow:hidden`, but its content (handle, title, chips, counts, two buttons) is about 222 px, so the buttons were cut in half at the tab bar. As the Owner asked, the peek no longer draws the buttons: it shows title, chips and counts (180 px at normal text, taller with large text, never clipped), and "Open checkpoint" and "Review cards" come with the pulled-up sheet. The map's legend and controls follow the sheet's measured height (`--v7-atlas-sheet-h`) instead of a fixed 180 px. The existing 180 px assertion in `v7-atlas.spec.ts` is unchanged and passes.
- **Calendar › "Browse day" ruler.** It floated over the bottom of the day grid, covering the last hour and the now line. On a phone it is now part of the dock: fixed on top of the tab bar, as wide as it, with matching rounded top corners and the tab bar's top corners squared, so the two read as one piece. It reads where the bar really starts (`--v7-dock-top`, measured, because the bar's height varies with text size), and the shell's `--v7-dock-height` grows by the ruler's 92 px, so the page's bottom padding keeps the whole day above it. It hides while a sheet is open, like the dock. Wide screens (the side rail) keep it in the grid.
- **Tests.** `tests/e2e/v7-n161-layout.spec.ts` checks all three at 360 and 390 px, and with text at 130% and 160%; all 12 cases fail on the code before this change. `v7-atlas`, `v7-part4`, `v7-part6`, `v7-n157-finish`, `v7-n138-touch`, `v7-n134-text-scale` and `v7-n159-release` pass. One run of `v7-part4` "moves by week" hit the date-dependent flake of bug #1389 under parallel load; it passed 3 of 3 on re-run.
