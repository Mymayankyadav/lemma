## N213 Time selection

The kit ruler supports time and duration controls with spoken values and keyboard steps. The capacity painter shows seven bars in owner-selected week order. Copy last week applies any dated capacity overrides from the previous week over the recurring weekday minutes. Saves retain their existing version and owner checks.

Exact time entry accepts HH:MM or four digits, alongside one-minute ruler steps and quarter-hour presets. Task estimates retain arbitrary whole-minute values under Exact estimate. Time logs retain 25/50/90-minute presets and custom duration; focus logs retain precise minute adjustment. Enter in the time sheet applies the time without submitting its parent form.
