## N213 Time selection

The kit ruler supports time and duration controls with spoken values and keyboard steps. The capacity painter shows seven bars in owner-selected week order. Copy last week applies any dated capacity overrides from the previous week over the recurring weekday minutes. Saves retain their existing version and owner checks.
