## N229 Tutor composer, transcript and drawing controls

- The composer eye hides or shows the title, progress strip and pinned question. The chat keeps the system-bar inset in either state. Scrolling and idle time leave the header visibility alone.
- The composer uses unboxed keyboard and attachment controls, a filled Send button and a quiet input surface. The reference screenshot measures about 28 px of text ink, 32 px for the add glyph and 26 px for the send arrow. At a 16 px composer font, the Lemma tool and send SVGs are 22 px and 18 px, with separate touch targets.
- Upload image and Take photo open the platform pickers directly. Attach context opens the context controls. Writing assistance, Refresh suggestions, Hint, Stuck, notation help and typing undo/redo are in the header's Session actions.
- An unfinished drawing has a highlighted composer icon. Tap to reopen it. Double-tap to discard the unfinished ink. The scratchpad also supports discarding through its actions menu. Inserted attachments stay in the draft.
- A rejected card proposal previously showed its saved summary and status below the reply. The chat now omits rejected proposals and the visible Tutor turn captions. Pending proposals retain their review links.
- The Markdown renderer already parsed numbered answers as a list. The app reset hid the markers while the chat retained list indentation and spacing. Chat now shows list markers in a compact gutter and aligns wrapped text under its item.
- Two moving fingers pan the scratchpad in both directions. Three fingers scroll the chat when the pad is inline, and pan the paper in full screen. A stationary second finger leaves a stroke in progress.
- Android full-screen paper follows the system viewport and reserves space for system bars and cutouts. The browser's landscape control stays inside the safe area. Existing ink keeps its paper coordinates when the view changes.
- While a tutor reply is pending, the learner can queue an inserted drawing or photo. The app saves the answer locally and sends it as the next turn once the tutor has replied and the network is available. Text follow-ups continue to join the waiting turn.
- Long-press a tutor reply or learner message to select it. Tap individual messages to add or remove them from the selection, then copy in conversation order or confirm deletion. The transcript collapses reply actions during selection and uses the same 16 px gap between speakers in either direction.
- Confirmed deletions follow the signed-in owner across devices, including earlier loaded history on refresh. They remove the selected messages from the conversation while retaining turn receipts, any handoff in progress and accepted study records. The demo remembers deletions in IndexedDB.
- The deletion table has a column-by-column capability inventory covering owner writes, export and account cleanup.
