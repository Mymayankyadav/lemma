## N131 The rest of the assets plan, aligned with v7 UX plan r3 (12 Nov 2026)

The Owner asked for the remaining A tasks (A3–A5) in one PR, then for them to follow plan r3 (N130,
`docs/v7/ux-audit-2026-11-12/`), which supersedes the release-and-assets plan where they differ. Built on N130's branch.

What exists now:

- **L-07 stamps** (r3 §6.2, in code): a decision made with the Approve or Reject button, or the A, R and arrow keys,
  presses the stamp onto the card and settles its last 4° (220 ms). The card leaves after 360 ms. A swipe already shows
  the stamp growing with the drag, so it leaves at once. Batch decisions and reduced motion skip the stamp.
- **L-09 loader** (r3 §7, "an emblem glyph drawing itself"): `Loader` draws a small emblem glyph in its construction
  order and lifts it again (1.6 s a cycle), beside the words saying what is loading. It replaces the plain status
  lines on Atlas, Focus, Round complete, the review round and Close the day. Under reduced motion the glyph stands
  complete.
- **L-16 theme transition** (r3 §7, "a cross-fade"): switching Day and Night in Settings cross-fades over 240 ms as a
  View Transition (`turnTheme` in `src/styles/appearance.ts`). It is instant without View Transitions, under reduced
  motion or Reduce effects, and when the theme does not change.
- **L-20 splash** (r3 §7, "a still on the web"): `index.html` puts the ℓ in a compass circle on the page colour inside
  `#root` until React replaces it. It has no animation.
- **L-04 focus orbit** (r3 §7, "prototype it in SVG first"), behind the flag `focusOrbit`, off by default. In an
  open-mode session the ring fills once per focused minute and empties at once at the turn. Paused, it dims and rests.
  Drifted sends one ripple. Under reduced motion there is no moving point. Rive is not added.
- **Sounds** (r3 §6.3), off by default: Settings › Appearance › Sounds, saved with the account (`settings.ui.sounds`).
  The three sounds are a pencil tick on grading, a page turn when a round completes and a low chime when the day
  closes. Each lasts at most 200 ms and is synthesised with Web Audio, so there are no audio files.
- **A5′ "Share the page"** (r3 §8): a 1200 × 630 notebook page with the ℓ emblem, the numbers and the ∎, drawn on
  demand by `src/ui/v7/shareImage.ts`, which is loaded only when asked. It is offered in three places:
  - Day closed: "Share the page", which also stops the walk back to Today;
  - Progress at 7 days: "Share this week";
  - a finished path: ⋯ › "Share this path".
  It goes to the system share sheet where the browser can share files, and is saved as a PNG otherwise. The site's
  own preview, `public/og-image.png` with Open Graph tags, is drawn by the same module (`scripts/brand/og-image.mjs`).
- **Path map:** the drawn map is reverted at the Owner's request ("still looking ugly"). The Path keeps the checkpoint
  list, as before. r3 orders the path spine (U3, V26) before any drawn map.

- the Atlas living sky: after U3;
- the 1.75 px icon strokes: wait for E21;
- a custom "copied" mark: r3 keeps the existing toast with its tick;
- G0's install screenshots and v7 pre-paint colours: their own item, since `check-pwa.mjs` and the layout tests pin the
  current values.
