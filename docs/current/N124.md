## N124 A1 Motion v1 (Owner task, 12 Nov 2026)

Phase A1 of the release and assets plan ("drawn with a compass": lines stroke in, surfaces fade and rise a few points,
nothing bounces, one thing moves at a time). No new library: CSS plus the existing `motion` package.

**What exists now**
- **Tokens** (`src/ui/v7/motion.css`, `motionV1` in `src/ui/v7/motion.ts`): instant 90 ms, quick 160 ms, base 240 ms,
  draw 700 ms on cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1), the sheet spring (stiffness 380, damping 36) as a CSS `linear()` easing
  that settles in 350 ms with no overshoot (`springEasing`), stagger 30 ms capped at six items, rise at most 12 px.
  The kit's existing tokens (`--v7-fast`, `--v7-base`…) are unchanged.
- **Reduce effects works.** Before, Settings › Appearance › Reduce effects only set an attribute nothing read, and only
  until reload. Now (`src/ui/v7/effects.ts`) it stops every animation and transition, blur and the page glow; it is
  mirrored on the device and applied before first paint, and follows the account setting when settings load. Every v7
  motion check (`motionReduced()` / `useMotionReduced()`) honours it together with the system's reduced motion: Toast,
  Focus, Close the day, Atlas and the kit primitives.
- **Sheets and dialogs** rise 12 px and fade in on the sheet spring (desktop: scale from .98); the backdrop fades.
  Closing stays instant.
- **List insert** (`useEnterMarks`): tasks and Calendar Day blocks added after the list loaded (Capture, quick add, an
  accepted suggestion, Load more) rise in, 30 ms apart; a list never animates on load, and a new day starts over.
- **Task complete**: the tick draws (240 ms), then the task is saved and leaves To do. Under reduced motion it saves at
  once, as before; a failed save un-draws the tick.
- **Review**: the answer rises in and the grade buttons fade in on reveal.
- **Calendar**: a lifted block grows to 1.02 while dragged.
- **Emblems** stroke in on the draw curve (the duration stays the kit's scenic 900 ms).

**Gate.** Every new animation sits in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` under
`html[data-v7]:not([data-v7-reduce-effects])` (checked by `tests/unit/v7/motion-v1.test.tsx`). Browser tests run with
reduced motion by default, so existing suites and captures are untouched; `tests/e2e/v7-motion.spec.ts` turns motion on
and checks the sheet, review and tick animations, their absence under both reduced settings, and an identical settled
sheet in all three modes.

**Not done in A1**
- Exit animations (closing sheets, removed rows): the plan's "nothing waits on an exit"; revisit with A3/A4.
- Inbox approve/reject stamps already follow the swipe; button decisions do not stamp.
- The plan's 4×-CPU frame check is not a blocking test (timing checks never block here); measure it in perf-report.
- Night-only ambient loops, the focus orbit and celebrations belong to A3/A4.

**Cost.** `motion.css` is 4.4 KB (1.5 KB gzipped) in the kit stylesheet; `effects.ts` and `useEnterMarks.ts` are small
modules. No new dependency.
