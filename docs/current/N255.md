## N255 · Settings paper and sky

In Settings › Appearance, **Paper by day** and **Sky by night** each control the background behind the app. A fresh installation uses the existing 24 px graph in both themes, now reported as Graph by both pickers. Selecting Plain by day or Clear by night removes it immediately. Dot, Ruled and Stars paint at their intrinsic tile sizes; in particular, the night sky is no longer squeezed from 160 px to 24 px. Choices are retained per device and do not overwrite the inactive theme.

The Reduce effects switch hides decoration without discarding either choice. Where account preferences do not supply a value, reopening Appearance shows the actual device switch state.

The production browser regression uses the Settings controls and samples screenshot pixels in an empty page margin. Before the fix, the supposedly blank backgrounds spanned 23 red-channel levels by night and 18 by day. Coverage includes reloads, theme switching, restoration of the Graph option and the Reduce effects override. No drawing surface or graph data changed.
