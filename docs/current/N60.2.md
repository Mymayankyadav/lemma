## N60.2 day-plan adapters — persistent owner-dated planning

N60.2 completes the N60.1 typed day-plan model with a mode-aware getDayPlanApi facade, a tab-isolated sessionStorage demo fixture, and an RLS-backed live adapter. A per-owner civil date is unique; a draft/preview may be updated, previewed placements preserve placed/unplaced/carried_over dispositions and reasons, and close/reopen only affect the selected date.

Demo and live list pages use bounded 1–50 keyset cursors scoped to filters and exact counts. Live reads only through owner RLS and performs create/update/preview/commit/close/reopen exclusively through day_plans_command with fresh cryptographic operation IDs reused on uncertain retries. Each successful receipt is verified against an owner readback. The server's registered N46.2 commit rechecks task and block source versions under database locks; the browser does not impersonate that atomic check.
