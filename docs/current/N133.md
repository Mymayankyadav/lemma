## N133 v7 UX audit r3: the rest of AN1 and G0 (12 Nov 2026)

The Android app's remaining AN1 items that need no Owner decision, and the stale assets of G0. Ticked in
`docs/v7/ux-audit-2026-11-12/CHECKLIST.md`.

- **AW5 Welcome sheet** (`src/platform/NativeWelcome.tsx`). Its three points rendered as one block: the sheet relied on
  the sign-in stylesheet, which is not loaded on Today. It now has its own (`native-welcome.css`) and reads as three
  rows with glyphs (Focus on the lock screen, Share to Lemma, Study mode and calendar), then "Lemma asks about
  notifications after your first focus session" and, last, the note for people who used Lemma before. The title is
  "Welcome" (no word left alone on a line), and the sheet opens with focus on its text, so no button shows a ring.
- **AW9 Study mode in one trip** (`src/platform/studyMode.ts`, `PhoneSettings.tsx`). When the switch sends the learner
  to Android's "Do Not Disturb access", the app remembers the request for ten minutes (`studyModeRequestedAt`); coming
  back with access granted turns Study mode on and says so.
- **AW13 The drift question** (`src/platform/drift.ts`, `src/features/focus/v7/DriftCard.tsx`). "Were you drifting?"
  was a 10-second toast with one action and no "No". The app now leaves the question for this visit, and Focus shows it
  as a quiet card ("You were away for 6 min. Were you drifting?") with **No** and **Yes, note it**, until answered.
- **G0 Pre-paint colours.** `index.html` painted the v6 papers before the app loaded. They now come from the build's v7
  flag (`src/app/paper.ts`, filled in by a small plugin in `vite.config.ts`): #F8F5F0 / #091023 for v7 builds, which
  are the default and the Android app; the legacy papers for builds without v7. `scripts/check-pwa.mjs` checks the pair
  that matches the build's install colour.
- **G0 Install screenshots.** `public/screenshots/today-{day,night}-1080x1920.png` were pre-v6. They are now v7 Today,
  captured from the real app by `scripts/v7/install-shots.mjs` (360 × 640 at 3×, example workspace, clock fixed),
  labelled "Today · Day" and "Today · Night". A wide screenshot was not added: `check-pwa.mjs` allows exactly the two.

Not in this item: AW14 icon layers and P7 (decision E23), AW15 naming (a wording decision), AW17 (needs a device).
