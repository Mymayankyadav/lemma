## N245 Exam study blocks in the owner agenda

An explicitly active sitting produces a splittable exam study candidate. The owner agenda chooses the legal time window with its existing calendar, capacity, reservation and commitment rules. The shared exam planner then picks a feasible action for that slot. Today and the MCP queue receive the same reason, next action, slot length, overall minutes remaining and fallback.

The exam cutoff belongs to its candidate. A pause or finish removes the candidate on the next read. Course deadlines, prerequisite edges, card schedules and manual deferrals retain their owner-set values. The post-deadline course queue can offer a feasible active exam block. Fixed appointments and zero available capacity still protect the learner’s time. A schedule suggestion writes no Calendar reservation.
