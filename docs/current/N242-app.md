## N242 Exam tutor app

A Path ledger or Today exam card opens the sitting-scoped tutor conversation behind `examMode`. Ordinary checkpoint transcripts remain separate and offer a dismissible link while exam prep is active. The exam header opens a read-only ledger. Session and composer actions can pause prep, finish or postpone the exam, and update the remaining study time.

The reply row supports saving a retrieval idea, undo, and finding ideas that the tutor saved. Learners can choose a short check or a recap. Attempt badges display independence, scores and time when the tutor has recorded them, and offer a prefilled score challenge. The timer runs while the tab is visible and prep is active. Sending after the timer expires still submits the answer with elapsed time and overtime metadata.

Demo chat imports can supply an explicit tutor grade for a blueprint row. The app does not assign a score based on a learner answer or elapsed time. Browser coverage exercises the separate transcript, timer, idea undo, exposure badge and budget update.
