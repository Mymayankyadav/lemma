## N230 Apple Pencil and palm rejection

The scratch pad and equation composer use the same Pointer Events canvas, inline and full screen. Apple Pencil is handled as `pointerType: pen`; there is no iOS user-agent routing, touch-to-pen guess, pressure/size threshold, or native Android change.

- Pen-down takes priority over an unfinished touch ink/eraser preview and discards that preview without altering committed strokes or Undo history.
- While a pen interaction is active, touch cannot draw, erase or pan, even if the pen is held stationary for longer than 800 ms. The existing 800 ms rejection window is refreshed when the pen ends or is cancelled.
- Touch contacts present at takeover, or first seen during rejection, remain rejected until their up/cancel/lost-capture event. Their movement cannot restart a pan after the pen lifts.
- Finger-only drawing, a stationary second finger, two moving fingers panning, three-finger navigation, mouse input, coalesced pen samples, full-screen transforms and existing interrupted-stroke recovery remain intact. Palm rejection also works for canvas callers without a pan handler.

### Validation boundary

Unit tests dispatch pointer sequences through the actual React component. Browser tests dispatch synthetic Pointer Events through both Tutor entry points, inline and full screen. Existing Chromium touch/CDP tests separately exercise gesture recovery. None of these is a physical iPad/Apple Pencil test or a guarantee about Safari's OS-level event ordering.

Input classification relies on the browser identifying Pencil as `pen`. A touch already committed or cancelled **before the first pen event** is retained, because existing Android/finger interruption recovery deliberately commits cancelled ink and erasing. The app cannot safely distinguish that historical contact from intentional finger input; this change does not retroactively erase it or delay all touch persistence. Palm-only contact before Pencil is detected may therefore leave a mark. Unknown/mouse input retains its existing behavior.

### Physical iPad acceptance still required

On supported iPad Safari with Apple Pencil, test both Tutor papers inline and full screen (portrait and landscape): palm-before-pen including OS touch cancellation, pen-before-palm, stationary pen with multiple palm contacts, lift/recontact, eraser takeover, app interruption and resume, Undo, and subsequent intentional two/three-finger navigation. Confirm saved strokes, attached drawings and equation recognition retain only intended ink. Also smoke-test Android finger drawing and rotation. No physical-device validation has been performed in this PR.
