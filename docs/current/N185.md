## N185 Atlas v3 activation safety

Preparing to activate production (docs/current/N180.md step 8) found four problems that would have hit the Owner on the first day.

- **Fixture projects blocked activation.** The post-deploy selftest creates `zz-selftest-*` projects (`projects.system`) in the Owner's account for minutes at a time; three existed at 18:30 on 2026-11-18. `atlas_check_release_report` asked each for a conversion. `20261218000900_n185_atlas_activation_fixtures.sql` replaces it (same signature and grants) to count only the Owner's projects (`not system`). The selftest itself is unaffected after activation: fixture projects get the legacy packet (`packet()` skips v2 for `system`), and the selftest only logs hint and note events, which write no attempts.
- **The v3 resume dropped the tutor's context.** It kept only NOW and Atlas cards in BRIEF and replaced the tutor rules, contrary to 04 §resume ("BRIEF uses card v2", rules "add"). It now keeps NOW, LAST, FIX, ADAPT, CARDS and SRC (Atlas cards in place of node cards) and the tutor rules with the Atlas lines appended. On the production export the largest resume is 1,394 tokens including OWNER (1,040 without), under the 1,500 budget.
- **Supply was 0 without planned blocks.** Feasibility counted only future study blocks in the calendar; the Owner has none but declares 300 weekday minutes. With no planned block, each study day up to `last_study_on` now contributes the declared minutes, split evenly among the projects still running that day, weighed by P(kept). On production this gives about 238 minutes per project to 22 Nov against 12,000–16,000 required: the projects really are infeasible, because completed 60-minute checkpoints took 151–549 minutes (pace about 3.8×).
- **v3 pruning ignored `auto_prune_standard`.** Below P_all 0.80 it deferred every not-started non-core checkpoint. Per 10 §5 it now defers venture material automatically, standard material only where the project allows it, and core never.
