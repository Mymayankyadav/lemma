## N160 — recurring tasks, their scheduler, and the map of the 30 chat-connector areas

**Owner task N160** ("Implement these", the 30-area checklist in `docs/mcp/OWNER_CHECKLIST.md`) is listed in `NEXT.md` with its remaining clusters N160.2 to N160.9. This PR is the hardest part end to end: areas 4 (recurring tasks) and the background half of 30, plus the map that verifies the connector against the server (area 1).

**What exists now**
- `public.task_series` (migration `20261213090000_n160_1_task_series.sql`): a rule (`daily`, `weekdays`, `weekly` with ISO weekdays and an interval, `monthly` with a day that clamps to the month end, `custom` every N days), a start and optional end date, skipped dates, a version, and `active`/`stopped`. Browsers can read it but cannot write it; `anon` and `authenticated` have INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE revoked.
- `public.tasks` gains `series_id` and `occurrence_date`. Occurrences are ordinary tasks (`due_kind='date'`, status `backlog`), so Today, Tasks, Calendar and the tasks MCP path show them with no change. A unique index on `(series_id, occurrence_date)` is what makes generation idempotent. The task command's patch whitelist does not include the two columns, so only the series command and the scheduler set them.
- `task_series_command` is the registered resource `task_series` with operations `create`, `update`, `skip`, `unskip`, `stop` and `resume`. Each has an `operationId` receipt, `expectedVersion` conflict checks (`status: conflict` with `current`), a journal action (`actionNo`) and undo. Through chat it is the existing `study` tool with `resource: "task_series"`; no new tool.
  - `create` fills the series from today to 14 days ahead. A series never creates tasks for past days (a daily task is not made overdue for days the app was closed).
  - `update` copies title, body, links, estimate and priority to open occurrences from today on (completed, cancelled and past ones are untouched). When the rule changes, untouched future occurrences the new rule no longer lands on are deleted; dates it still lands on keep their task. Undo restores them.
  - `skip` adds a date to `skipped_dates` and cancels that date's open task; `unskip` reverses it. `stop` ends the series and deletes untouched future occurrences (`keep_future: true` keeps them); `resume` generates again from today.
- Scheduler: `lemma_run_task_series()` (service_role only) is called by a `pg_cron` job every 10 minutes where `pg_cron` exists (the same install pattern as `lemma-run-due-commands`). One tick serves one owner (the journal allows one owner per transaction), claims series with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, and journals as source `system`, tool `task_series_scheduler`. Overlapping ticks, retries and undo cannot create a date twice.
- `docs/mcp/capability-map.json` maps the 30 areas to registered resources, operations and tools, and `tests/unit/mcp/capability-map.test.ts` fails when a named resource, operation or tool does not exist, when a registered resource belongs to no area, or when an unfinished area has no sub-item in `NEXT.md`.

**Not in this item** (the clusters N160.2 to N160.9 of the `NEXT.md` item): a recurring-task editor in the app (series appear as the tasks they generate), week/month planning, remote device control, and the other `partial` or `missing` areas of the map.

**Tests**: `tests/mcp/sql/task-series.mjs` (rules with fixed dates, create/edit/skip/stop/resume, undo, owner isolation, MCP bridge, scheduler without duplicates), the `task_series` round trip in `tests/mcp/sql/parity.mjs`, `tests/unit/mcp/capability-map.test.ts`, and the updated capability-registry JSON files (`task_series.json`, `tasks.json`, `lemma_command_resources.json`, `lemma_undo_tables.json`).
