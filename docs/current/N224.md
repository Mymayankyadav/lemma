## N224 One suggestion strip, equation mode as paper, the rich answer on, and the Owner's phone review

N223 (#1829) made equations in an answer rendered chips and added equation mode and the scratch pad's one row of tools. Using it on the phone, the Owner asked for the following.
- Gboard's strip, with the pencil at its left edge and the symbols inside the suggestion slots.
- Autoeq on what is typed in the chat box, with all suggestions in one place and no grey text or settings.
- Equation mode as paper for writing by hand, reading the whole equation only on request.
- One tap on the pencil for the scratch pad.
- A fix for the Today tutor time, calmer tutor text, and tidier Atlas, prerequisites, context and card-draft screens.

This replaces N223's composer, which was framed by operation, variable, digit and symbol keys around a composition line. It also replaces N223's pencil tap and double tap, Hide equation keys and Search symbol. This note describes what ships now.

### The answer: equations as chips (`tutorScratch` on)

- **Chips.** Every `$…$` or `$$…$$` in the answer is an atomic chip that renders its TeX with KaTeX (`TutorAnswerEditor.tsx`).
  - The draft is still stored as plain `$…$` text, so sending, copying and history are unchanged.
  - An unfinished `$` stays text. The caret moves around a chip as one character.
- **Editing.** Tapping a chip opens the Equation sheet: a live preview, the LaTeX, **Done**, **Write by hand** (equation mode, whose reading replaces it) and **Remove**.
- **The flag.** The rich answer, the pencil's scratch pad and image attachments were all behind `tutorScratch`, which was off in production. The Owner's phone therefore showed raw `$x^{1}+y^{2}$` and no pencil.
  - The flag is now on by default (Owner decision; a flag turned on by default needs an independent review).
  - `?flags=-tutorScratch` keeps the plain text answer in dev, test and e2e builds. The specs that test that path pin it.

### One strip for every suggestion

The strip always has Gboard's frame:
- Draw symbol (✎) at the left edge, or Next □ while a □ is empty;
- Equation mode (√) at the right edge;
- three equal slots between them, the best in the middle.

The slots show the first of these:
1. **Autoeq's readings of an equation just typed in prose** (`typedEquation.ts`, `useAutoeqReadings.ts`).
   - `Let x1 + x2 = 4` offers x₁+x₂=4, x¹+x²=4 and 1x+2x=4. The words before it rank them: "The circle" puts powers first.
   - N222's local rules answer at once. Its on-device autoeq model (4.2 MB, Lemma's own verified files) is asked only when they have nothing.
   - The equation as written comes last. A tap replaces the typed span with an equation chip.
   - The detection stops at the first word, needs a sign or an indexed variable (x1), ends on a term rather than a sign still being typed, and ignores maths already inside `$…$`.
2. **Symbols for a LaTeX command, short form or name.** `\al`, `->`, `RR` and also `eps` and `mu`, whose symbol used to hide in the icon's place.
3. **The word suggestions.**
4. **With nothing to suggest, ∀ ∈ ⇒.** At a word boundary with next words, the words have the slots and the symbols are not squeezed beside them.

The fix and Undo cards for a spelling or notation fix are unchanged.

**Always on, no settings, no grey text.**
- Live accounts used to default to Off, so they saw no suggestions at all, symbols included.
- Without the new `writingAidSettings` flag (off), every account gets Balanced suggestions and the stored mode is ignored. Settings and the composer's Answer actions no longer show Writing assistance.
- The grey-text continuation is behind `proofAssistGhost` (off), for the later proof assist.
- `tests/e2e/tutor-writing-aid.spec.ts` and two unit files turn both flags on, so the settings and the continuation stay tested.
- **Refresh suggestions** is in Answer actions (⋯). It applies once the menu closes, to the words the strip shows again.

### Equation mode: paper in the keys' place

√ (or Write by hand on a chip) replaces only the keys. The band under them shrinks to the gesture inset.
- **Strip.** It keeps its frame: Keys at the left, √ pressed at the right, and the readings between.
- **Header row.** A slim row holds **Read**, **Undo stroke** and **Clear writing**.
- **Paper.** The rest is paper: a sheet 2.5 times the window's width and twice its height. It scrolls both ways with two fingers, a wheel or the scroll bars (`InkCanvas` `panTouches`).
- **Single symbols** are read as they are drawn by Draw single symbols, when that setting is on.
  - The ink is scaled into its 320 × 116 paper and replayed. It never inserts on its own.
  - A tap inserts the symbol as an equation chip of its command (`$\forall$`).
- **The whole equation** is read by Pix2Text MFR only when the learner taps **Read** (Owner: "use manual trigger, not for detexify"). The first Read asks before the 118 MB download. A tap inserts the reading as a chip and clears the paper.
- **Latency.** Readings no longer start at every pause; each one used to run in full on the same single thread.
  - The worker now runs only the newest request. A superseded one stops at its next decoding step.
  - When the paper opens, a cached model starts loading (`prepareEquationModel`), so the first Read is not a cold start (reading and checking 118 MB, building two sessions).
  - WASM threads need a cross-origin-isolated page (SharedArrayBuffer). The app is not isolated today, so the worker uses up to four cores only when it is.
  - The model is unchanged.

### The pencil and the scratch pad

- **The pencil.** One tap on the composer's pencil (**Scratch**, as on main) opens the scratch pad; another closes it.
- **One row of tools.** Keys first, then Pen, Eraser, Hand, Undo stroke, Recognize equation, ⋯ and Insert drawing (an ↑ action button).
- **The paper.** The resize grip sits on the paper's top edge and the hint inside it. The paper scrolls both ways with the Hand.
- **Draw symbol** (✎ in the strip) is unchanged. On landscape phones its choices stand beside its paper.

### Research behind the choices (web search, 22 Nov)

- **Typed shorthand.** Autoeq (3.3M parameters, Apache-2.0) is the only small on-device text-to-LaTeX model found.
- **Larger alternatives.** MathSpeech's 120M LM, or a 0.5–1.5B general model fine-tuned on MathBridge, would be hundreds of MB.
- **Handwriting.** ink-on (CoMER, 7.2 MB INT8, onnxruntime-web) is far smaller than Pix2Text MFR's 118 MB, but its licence and maturity are unconfirmed. professor-euler's MathWriting model is CC BY-NC-SA.
- **Not added.** No new dependency is added here.

### Review fixes (changes requested on #1830)

- **Read was stuck after drawing during a download.** New ink always returns the reader to idle, so Read is enabled again. The download carries on in the worker, and the learner's consent is remembered, so the next Read continues it without asking again.
- **A handwritten symbol did not replace the equation being written again.** Only control words (`\forall`) went in as equations; a symbol like `\mathbb{R}` went in as a glyph, outside the replacement. Every symbol read from handwriting now goes in as an equation of its LaTeX (`symbolLatex`, or the glyph where it has none). The equation chosen with Write by hand is replaced.
- **The equation as written could not be chosen.** With three autoeq readings (`x1+x2=4`), the as-written one was cut by the three slots. `slotReadings` keeps it in the third slot after the two best readings, unless a reading already is it.
- **Tests.**
  - e2e: drawing during a hanging download; a symbol replacing `$y$` through Write by hand; As written visible.
  - Unit: slot selection, and LaTeX for ∀ and ℝ.
