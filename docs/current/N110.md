## N110 Mockup shell, with the N111–N117 screen ports (one PR, Owner direction 2026-11-08)

Everything below applies only with `v6Shell` on; flag-off screens, their tests and visual rules are unchanged (`Screen`, `TabBar`, `AppShell` and the Today/Focus components branch on the flag).

**Look (N110).** `src/ui/v6/mockup.css` is generated from `docs/v6/prototype/source/`: part 1 is the focus-overlay, dock and `cr-button` rules of `owners/design-system.css`, part 2 is `mockup-overrides.css` unchanged. Every selector is prefixed with `:root[data-v6]` (`html[data-v6]` for root rules), so a re-port is a mechanical diff. Repo-specific bindings (shell spacing, Inbox action bar clearance, rem-based large-text reflow, landscape keeps the five-tab bar, route sheets keep the bar) live in `src/ui/v6/mockup-bindings.css`. `src/ui/v6/tokens.css` now uses the mockup palette (navy primary `#294C66` Day, `#AACCE5` Night; paper `#FFFDF8` / `#1C2733`), still meeting the N19 contrast test.

**Shell (N110).** `src/app/V6Header.tsx` renders the mockup page header: Today shows the ℓ mark, "Lemma" and the date; other screens show a large serif title, a Back control on detail routes, and the icon cluster (Inbox with badge, + Add menu, ⋯ menu) inside `nav[aria-label="Page actions"]`. Detail screens other than Cards and Inbox show only ⋯, whose menu then lists Inbox and Add (mockup `NotebookMenu` compact rule). `/atlas` is titled "Concept map". Add offers Task, Time block (Calendar) and New path; Escape closes an open header menu. `src/app/TabBar.tsx` (v6 only) is the labelled five-tab bar (`data-ui=main-navigation`); detail routes keep their destination highlighted (`/p/*` → Paths, `/cards*` and `/review/*` → Review). `src/app/FocusAccessory.tsx` is the mockup dock (`#cr-mobile-dock`) directly above the bar: intent, state and a ticking mm:ss clock (accessible name keeps "N min to phase deadline"), plus Pause/Resume; tapping it opens Focus. `src/app/v6Chrome.ts` measures the bar and dock into `--lemma-nav-height` / `--lemma-bottom-chrome`. The Demo chip and graph-paper ground are hidden under v6 (demo screens say "Example workspace" instead). Legacy `Screen` headers render only their actions under v6 (the title becomes an sr-only `h2`).
