## N206 Atlas controls

The map starts near the current checkpoint when that checkpoint resolves to a constellation. Search, the Map/List switch, compass, and Layers use compact controls. Recentre switches between the checkpoint constellation and the whole sky while keeping the selected concept. List groups the same concepts by subject. Opening a concept reveals its peek actions and evidence. The browser restores the previous layer after switching views, including after a reload. The Bridges layer appears after an approved bridge has a recorded use.

The peek keeps Continue, Review, Expand and Close reachable at 360 and 390 pixels, including with enlarged text.
