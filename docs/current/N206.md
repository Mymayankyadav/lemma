## N206 Atlas controls

The map starts near the current checkpoint when that checkpoint resolves to a constellation. Atlas options occupy one vertical rail on the right, with nearby subject shortcuts beneath the map controls. The rail floats above the content with a transparent background and individual translucent glass buttons. Its top eye button shows the options; the crossed-out eye hides them. Back stays separately available at the upper left. Search expands from its rail position and closes after a result is chosen. Recentre switches between the checkpoint constellation and the whole sky while keeping the selected concept. List groups the same concepts by subject. Opening a concept reveals its peek actions and evidence. The browser restores the previous layer after switching views, including after a reload. The Bridges layer appears after an approved bridge has a recorded use.

The peek keeps Continue, Review, Expand and Close reachable at 360 and 390 pixels, including with enlarged text.

Short screens scroll the rail beneath its pinned toggle and above the idea panel or navigation. Collapsing the rail returns space to an expanded idea panel. The rail floats above the map without shrinking its framing or changing the camera when toggled. Switching between Map and List preserves the chosen rail state and visible keyboard focus. Map-only controls remain unavailable while List covers the paused map.

When the open rail narrows a phone's List, due counts sit beneath the idea name instead of squeezing it between the count and chevron. The full-width collapsed List keeps its compact rows.
