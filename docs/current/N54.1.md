## N54.1 — app sync from the owner change feed

Live owner sessions now poll `changes_since` in pages of 100 while the tab is visible, with immediate refresh on visibility, focus and reconnect. Each session has a disposable account-scoped cursor; sign-out and account switch stop the poller and discard it. The server's reset marker invalidates all owner queries once while the returned cursor is retained, and full pages are drained before the ordinary 12-second interval. Failed RPCs retry with bounded exponential backoff; hidden tabs suspend polling. Demo sessions never subscribe.

`src/data/sync/keys.ts` is the single resource-to-query-key registry. Known resources invalidate only their mapped query prefixes; a new/unmapped resource invalidates every owner query instead of leaving a stale page. Auth and bootstrap coordination queries are excluded from bulk owner invalidation. `useAuth` attaches the subscription once to live owner identity; multiple hook consumers share a single poller. No production schema changes.
