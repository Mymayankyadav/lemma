## N234 Tutor answer aids over MCP (flag `tutorAnswerAids`, default off)

The tutor knows the question, so it can say which phrases are safe boilerplate and which words would give the answer away. With its
reply it may attach **answer aids** for the learner's next answer.

```
study {schemaVersion:1, resource:"tutor_answer_aids", target:{id:<handoff ID>}, operationId:<fresh UUID>, operation:"set",
  patch:{level:"scaffold"|"light"|"none", wording:[≤24 phrases ≤80 chars, no $ \ ^ _ = { } < >], terms:[≤40 words],
         symbols:[≤16 short expressions], withhold:[≤24 words or phrases]}}
get(what=tutor_answer_aids, id=<handoff ID>)
```

- **Contract.** Migration `20261218002700_n234_tutor_answer_aids.sql` (additive: one table, owner-only functions, browsers' writes revoked
  as bug #1094 requires, account-deletion guard, export registration). `tutor_answer_aid_connector` (service role, owner connector secret and the
  handoff grant, only on a turn this grant answered) takes `set` (creates, or replaces the turn's aids with the next version, idempotent) and
  `list`. `tutor_answer_aid_state` (authenticated) is the learner's read. Validation exists twice and must agree: `tutor_answer_aid_valid` in SQL and
  `answerAidIssues` in `supabase/functions/_shared/tutor-answer-aids.ts`. Wording and terms may not contain a withheld term.
  Registry: `docs/v6/capability-registry/tutor_answer_aids.json`. The handoff instructions carry `ANSWER_AID_NOTE`.
  **This migration deploys to production on merge, so it needs an independent review under AGENTS.md. It has not been run against any database.**
- **Keyboard** (`writingAid/answerAids.ts`, `engine.ts`, `TutorComposer.tsx`). Terms and symbols join the decoder's context (they rank higher and
  count as words the learner may restate). At level `scaffold` the tutor's wording is offered one word at a time (a phrase continues when the
  sentence's last k words are its first k; k ≥ 2 or the whole sentence so far; an opener is offered once a letter is typed). Withheld words are
  never offered, at any prefix and at any level. The existing "two accepted words, then learner evidence" guard still applies to bank words.
- **Fading.** `effectiveAidLevel`: the tutor's level is a ceiling; mastery ≥ 0.6 lowers scaffold to light, ≥ 0.85 to none. Mastery is a stand-in
  (a demonstrated checkpoint 0.9, otherwise the share of steps done × 0.6) until the Atlas's evidence is passed in.
- **Demo.** The demo tutor carries a sample bank (`DEMO_ANSWER_AID` in `DemoCheckpointTutor.tsx`); a live thread uses the aids from `tutor_answer_aid_state`.
- **Not done:** tagging accepted bank text in the turn's `answerParts` (it needs a change to the turn contract); automatic banks from the question when no
  tutor sets one; the SQL, MCP unit and registry tests.
