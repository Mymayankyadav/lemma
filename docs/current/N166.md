## N166 Emblem system v3: the subject emblems redrawn as ink line drawings from one generator

Owner task (2026-11-15): audit the supplied emblem set (`lemma-emblems.zip`, "reference-matched SVG edition"), make the emblems more beautiful and more in keeping with Lemma, and update them in the app.

### Audit of the supplied set

| Finding | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| 30 SVGs weighing 16.1 MB; the Analysis hero alone is 5.7 MB, Topology 2.5 MB, even the Algebra and Topology 20 px glyphs are 37–54 KB | Unshippable in the PWA, the Android bundle or a share image; `emblemPng` would stall a phone. |
| Analysis, Algebra, Topology and the torus are potrace tracings of the concept bitmaps: filled outlines, not strokes | They cannot draw themselves in (A1, L-09 loader), ignore `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`, and do not fit the `EmblemStroke` contract the component, exporter and tests share. |
| Gradients and translucent fills in the Geometry, Logic, Probability and Combinatorics heroes | The brand spec (R2 §1, §2) says no gradients; fills band on Android and fight the Night glow. |
| Three illustration styles in one set: shaded 3-D plastic, flat line diagrams, traced engraving | The set did not read as one hand; the Day/Night recolour only worked for some of it. |
| Tiers that do not share a silhouette: Logic hero is a cube but its tile and glyph are nested diamonds; Geometry hero is a shaded sphere, its glyph a six-petal flower | The spec's rule that a glyph is the family's silhouette was broken. |
| Glyph collisions and stray symbols at 20 px: Number theory's glyph is a hexagram, Probability's six dots read as a loading spinner, Geometry's flower and Other's rose look alike | Chips and lists stop telling subjects apart; a religious symbol is not a modular times table. |
| Number theory hero (×7 mod 120) and the Topology/Analysis meshes go muddy at 164 px | Hero detail must survive the native size, not only the 520 px focus view. |

### What changed

`src/ui/v7/emblem.ts` is rewritten; `Emblem`, `emblemExport` and every caller are unchanged. The supplied concept families are kept where they read well, drawn honestly from the mathematics rather than traced:

| Subject | Kind | Hero | Tile | Glyph |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analysis | `radial-wave` (was `partial-sums`) | standing wave on a disc in 3-D, hidden lines removed; focal = the profile through the centre | circle, profile, a fainter later phase | circle and profile |
| Algebra | `icosahedron` (was `mapped-plane`) | the solid down a 2-, 3- or 5-fold axis, faces turned from the light engraved at 30°; the arrows are that rotation | front edges and silhouette | 3-fold view (hexagon of triangles) |
| Topology | `torus-knot` | (2, q) knot as a tube with lines along and rings across it, real alternating over/under gaps | tube edges and core | core with crossing gaps |
| Number theory | `multiplication-circle` | ×k mod n chords banded by length so the envelope emerges, residue ticks | 32-point table | cardioid in its circle (no hexagram) |
| Geometry | `euclid` | Euclid I.1 carried on to the triangular lattice of compass points | two circles, AB, triangle | vesica and triangle |
| Logic | `hasse` | B₃ or B₄; edges that add the same element share an opacity; focal = a maximal chain with its nodes | B₃ | B₂ diamond of four nodes |
| Probability | `markov` (was `galton`) | 4–6 states: forward transitions focal, backward bowed in, self-loops, a few jumps | 3–4 states | the two-state chain |
| Combinatorics | `complete-graph` | Kₙ, edges grouped by reach (shorter stronger), stopping at the nodes | K₆/K₇ | K₅ (the only star in the set) |
| Other | `rose` | the rose as a ribbon of nested roses in its guide circle | rose, inner rose, circle | rose |

Rules kept exactly: tier budgets (hero ≤ 160, tile ≤ 24, glyph ≤ 3), widths, one focal stroke at 100%, context at 40–60% (now chosen per stroke for depth: nearer 60%, farther 40%), drawing order, the 200-unit frame inside the r = 98 clip, `currentColor`, M/L/A/Z paths only (so `pathLength` still times every stroke), deterministic per seed, seed varies parameters never the family. The sign-in `compass` construction draws the same as before.

Not changed: the Other family stays the rose (the torus in the supplied set was a study and would echo Topology); no bitmaps, fills, gradients or new dependencies; component, CSS and exporter untouched.

### Verification

- The whole set (27 subject × tier SVGs) is now about 110 KB instead of 16.1 MB; the largest hero path is 25 KB.
- `tests/unit/v7/emblem.test.ts`: the three renamed kinds updated and the family × tier snapshot regenerated for the new drawings; no assertion removed or loosened. Every assertion was also replayed outside Vitest over 9 subjects × 3 tiers × 64 seeds (budgets, one focal, widths, 40–60%, no NaN, every point inside the clip or glyph box, `pathLength` > 0).
- Typechecked with the repo's compiler options. Hero plus tile compute in ≤ 6 ms median per subject (cached after).
- Rendered in Day and Night at 164 / 48 / 20 px; the nine glyphs are distinct at 20 px. Before/after: `docs/current/N166-before-after.png`.
