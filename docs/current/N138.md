## N138 v7 UX audit r3: every control takes a 44 px tap (U2 · V36) (13 Nov 2026)

The audit measured the true hit area of every control at 360 and 390 px; six kinds were under 44 px (V36). Each now
takes a tap 21 px from its centre in every direction (`tests/e2e/v7-n138-touch.spec.ts`), with Agenda's "Add" too:

- **Invisible hit areas** (`.v7-badge-action::after` in `kit-system.css`, path cards' "14 due", Calendar's "Adjust")
  are now the touch size tall, centred on the control (`top:50%; height:max(100%, var(--v7-touch))`), so they are
  44 px on the web and 48 px in the app whatever the control's border; insets from the padding box lost the border's
  pixel at each edge ("14 due" reached 42 px). "Adjust" sits above the day grid that followed it.
- **"1 overdue · Triage"** sits in Calendar's lane, a horizontal scroller, which clipped its hit area to 28 px. The lane
  now leaves room for it (padding, cancelled by an equal negative margin, so nothing moves).
- **Hub tabs** ("Due", "Log", "Time", "Atlas") are at least the touch size wide.
- **Time log.** The week's arrows took 96 px beside the day strip, leaving each day 31 px at 360 px. They now sit on
  either side of the week's name ("‹ 9–15 November ›", `weekRangeLabel`), as in Calendar, and the strip has the whole
  width: 45 px a day at 360 px.
- **Almanac** (Progress). Its 24 px cells stay (a dense calendar), but the grid is now one touch target, like a slider:
  a tap anywhere picks the day under the finger, a tap in a gap the nearer day (`heatmapIndexAt`), and a mouse picks as
  it moves. The cells keep their single roving tab stop for keys and screen readers.

Not here: Calendar Week's day headers and blocks at 360 px (40 px columns are a layout question for U1/U3) and the
Inbox list's checkboxes.
