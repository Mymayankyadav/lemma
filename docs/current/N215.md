## N215 Progress and goals

Overview shows focused time, subjects, and goals. Prior-period comparisons require prior activity. Learning and Log remain available, and the Week view shows a labelled daily chart. Planning supports goal editing and checks for an existing goal with the same period, dates and scope.

The populated Study almanac remains below Goals, including when the selected day has no study time. Its summary gives the number of study days in the visible period. Range selection stays available in empty ranges. Progress actions retain goal creation, weekly sharing and a details sheet with review/recall/checkpoint counts, subject history and confidence details. Hard, Good and Easy keep the grayscale treatment with wider luminance separation.
