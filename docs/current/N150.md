## N150 Inbox keys from the first moment (bug #1375, 13 Nov 2026)

`tests/e2e/v7-inbox.spec.ts` › "five proposals can be decided by keys alone … and E opens the editor" failed about one
run in three on main. The key was not slow; it was lost. Timestamps from instrumented runs showed three windows:

1. The Inbox attached its keydown listener in a `useEffect`, which runs after paint, and re-attached it on every change
   of the top card. A key pressed between the Inbox's first render and that effect reached no listener.
2. While the proposals were loading there was no top card, so the listener ignored E.
3. Under load, Playwright's `networkidle` resolved before the Inbox's code had even been requested. The E arrived
   100–980 ms before any Inbox code was in the page.

What exists now:
- **`src/features/proposals/v7/useInboxKeys.ts`** attaches one listener synchronously at mount (`useLayoutEffect`) and
  reads the latest state through a ref. A, R and the arrows decide, E edits, and Up skips, as before.
- **An E pressed early** is kept and opens the editor when the first card shows, within 5 s
  (`PENDING_MS`). That covers an E pressed while the stack loads, and an E pressed on the Inbox route before the Inbox
  has mounted.
- **`src/features/proposals/v7/inboxEarlyKeys.ts`** holds the early E. It is a few hundred bytes with no dependencies,
  and `src/app/routes.tsx` imports it, so its listener exists from the start. It keeps nothing while an Inbox is
  listening, so no key is handled twice.
- **A decision key (A, R, arrows) pressed before the cards show is dropped.** Nobody approves or rejects a card they
  have not seen.

Measured with the same build and runner: under a two-core load (4 workers, 40 runs) main failed 1 of 40 and N150
passed 40 of 40. Under a one-core load (6 workers, 30 runs) main passed 4 of 30 and N150 passed 27 of 30 with a 4 s
window; the window is now 5 s. The existing test is unchanged. Its remaining failures under that load come from E
pressed before the app's own code has run, which nothing in the page can see.
