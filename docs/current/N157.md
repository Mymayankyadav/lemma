### Android icons and names (IC4, IC6, AW14, AW15, AW16; G1's themed ∎; Owner decision E23)

- **One recipe for every launcher shortcut** (`scripts/android-icons.mjs`):
  - the selection tint (`lemma_tint`, from `selection.subtle`) with the glyph in its indigo (`lemma_tint_ink`, from
    `selection.fg`), as selected items look in the app;
  - adaptive on Android 8+ and a round tile on 7.1;
  - drawn from the same lucide glyphs the app shows: Review (Layers), Quick focus (Timer), Capture (Plus), Inbox
    (Inbox) and Resume *path* (Route, never the Atlas star).
- **Old icons:** the three PNGs copied from the web (`drawable-nodpi`) are gone. Inbox no longer uses the file named
  "approve" (AW16).
- **Review:** the dynamic "Review *n*" now replaces the static Review instead of repeating it. Review is always a dynamic
  shortcut ("Review", or "Review 12" when cards are due), next to the three static ones. `Shortcuts.java` allows two
  dynamic shortcuts next to three static.
- **Quick Settings tiles:** each has its own monochrome glyph, a timer for Focus and a plus for Capture. Before, both
  showed the ℓ.
- **Themed icon (Android 13+):** the ℓ and the ∎, both inside the launcher's 66 dp safe circle (the `themed` layout in
  `lemma-mark.mjs`). The grid and margin were already in the background layer (N137).
- **AW15:** one word for a planned block, the app's own ("New time block"). The notification channel and its Settings
  switch are now "Time blocks", as the plan asked ("rename the channel to match the app's word"). Two expectations that
  pinned "Study blocks" changed with it.
- **Check:** the Owner's device checks 6 and 9 (long-press the icon, Quick Settings, the themed icon on a dark
  wallpaper) look at these on a phone.
