## N220 One consistent v7 interface (Owner screenshots, 22 Nov 2026)

The Owner reviewed the N202 release on the phone and asked for every reported defect to be fixed in one PR. The fixes, by area:

- **Type and spacing.** The v7 reset made every `h1`–`h4` serif at the browser's sizes, so hosted forms, sheet titles and section headings mixed two families and odd sizes. Only page titles (`h1`) use the serif now; `h2`–`h4`, `.v7-headline`, `.v7-subhead` and sheet titles use the sans on the kit scale.
- **Sheets.** An eyebrow sits 4 px above a 20 px sans title (two lines at most), the Close button lines up with it, footer buttons share the row equally (the secondary is tonal, not a bare link), and the body and footer clear the gesture bar. Safe-area insets use `--v7-safe-top` and `--v7-safe-bottom`, the larger of `env()` and the `--safe-area-inset-*` values the Android app injects (older WebViews report `env()` as 0).
- **Calendar.** The header is every hub's header (title, Search, Inbox, then the Calendar ⋯). The Schedule and Tasks tabs sit beneath it. The view menu, Browse day and the period arrows share one toolbar row (`group "Calendar navigation"`). A dragged block now follows the finger: its offset counts the pointer's travel plus the grid's own scroll since the drag began, and the grid auto-scrolls smoothly near its edges. Before, it scrolled 18 px per move and the block slid away. The time block sheet no longer focuses the title on open. Its Start and End are typed 24-hour fields (`TimeField`; four digits apply at once), and its length is a choice of 25 min, 50 min, 1 h 30 min and 2 h. Quick add also uses presets. The three full-day rulers are gone from these sheets; Browse day keeps its ruler.
- **Atlas.** The rail has a button for every other subject (every subject when zoomed out). Before, it showed two neighbours. At a distance only the largest names are drawn. Around a focused constellation at most six names take a leader line, the rest appear on zoom, and labels keep out of the rail, Back and the peek. The peek fits a two-line title and floats above the gesture bar.
- **Path.** The emblem shows at full strength beside the title. The goal is one line that opens in place, where it used to be repeated beneath itself. Target date, time left and Set time are chips. Stage headers share one style, and sources are one list card.
- **Practice.** In How did it go?, the four ratings share the row, and elapsed time, the timer and Minutes line up on one centre line.
- **Progress and charts.** Your week sits with the period figure, which is now at a numeric size, not 64 px. Charts have no Table switch. Tapping a bar, row or day shows its values in a tip (floating just above the plot over the tapped bar on bar charts), and each chart keeps its data table for screen readers. Easy is blue (`viz.easy`, new role) instead of the pale indigo that read as grey.
- **Settings.** Group explanations moved behind an (i) beside the group title (`Group info`). Row hints that only restated the label are gone; Data and account's longer hints are (i) tips, and the Tutor route hints are one short line each. The time-zone note stays visible. Reminder kinds that only reach the Inbox are chips. The hosted advanced forms (study defaults, focus, activities) use the kit's sans headings, field and button sizes. An activity's Order field and its Save order sit together on their own line, and every field's label sits above it. The Writing aid group titles sit above their cards.
- **Answer keyboard and composer.** The bottom row follows Gboard: ?123 and Enter are a key and a half, comma, maths and full stop are one key each, and the space bar is four keys wide. ∀ ∈ ⇒ and Next □ moved to the suggestion strip. While a word is typed they step aside, so its suggestions get the strip. A pressed key's preview is larger and lingers 110 ms after a quick tap (none with reduced motion). Keys play Android's own keyboard-tap haptic (`LemmaNative.keyTap`, `HapticFeedbackConstants.KEYBOARD_TAP`; other platforms keep a 10 ms tick). Letters get tap correction: a touch on the outer part of a key counts for its neighbour when only the neighbour continues a word in the bundled word list, and a word's first letter is never changed. Drawn-symbol candidates are compact 36 px chips. The answer box reads like a chat app's composer: the text on top, with Keys or Scratch, ⋯ and Send on one row beneath. The Tutor header clears the status bar, and session parts wrap instead of being cut.

Test changes follow the Owner's decisions and keep each test's intent. There is no Show as table switch, and the screen-reader table carries the same rows. The block sheet opens without focusing the title. Its Length is a preset group and its Start and End are text fields. Calendar navigation is the toolbar row, and Calendar actions is in the header's Page actions. The Atlas rail is named Subjects and lists the other subjects without a cap. The Path goal opens in place without repeating itself. New unit tests cover tap correction and the typed time field.
