### Prospective local practice protocol

Before registration, the research operator records the participating course, bank and rubric versions, target population, recruitment and consent, assessor procedures, missing-data handling, and the planned sample size justified for that population. Use one frozen protocol ID and one 224-day enrollment horizon per track. The registration RPC accepts an in-scope randomized certificate. It writes a registration and an initial aggregate checkpoint.

1. Score twelve common intake questions before assignment. Match two consenting learners in the same 0–4, 5–7 or 8–12 stratum. Use one pair UUID and preserve learner order across assignment retries. The server randomizes one eligible challenger against native. Course-wide and intake-specific selections need 100 mature learners in each compared arm, a simultaneous transfer lower bound above .005 and burden upper bounds at most .025, .035, .085 and .045 for abandonment, triggering, feedback and idle time.
2. Measure delayed transfer on day 20. Store the separate assessor and reporting dates with the outcome RPC. Each immutable row also records its actual database acceptance time. On day 21 the due cohort can contribute on-time reports. Weekly checkpoints preserve fixed pair order and retain a release receipt after retry. Challenger practice covers elapsed days 0–13. A stale checkpoint, scope change, expired certificate, different intake, or missing monitoring service returns native.
3. Advance the checkpoint weekly. Initial weekly and pending limits are 24/72 pairs. Promotion uses the three-factor benefit mixture with wagers .25/.50/.80, margin .005 and threshold 40, raising limits to 64/144. A harm mixture at or above 1,280, less than 95% due-grade coverage in either arm, or an unavailable due observer closes enrollment. Continue collecting follow-up for enrolled learners. Starting another campaign requires a newly reviewed protocol and error budget.
4. Use previously verified item versions, approved scope and bank, and a current model snapshot. Store approved mappings in atlas_practice_bank. A changed item or expired binding withdraws its eligibility. A specialized arm requires its own valid, dated course score. When that score is unavailable the item selector uses native. A proof certificate requires independent qualifying demonstrations in the declared context. Only existing approval and study commands can record a demonstrated checkpoint.
