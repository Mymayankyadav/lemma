  (progress, 68 → 76) larger. The coordinates were scaled, so the line stays 3 units.

  drawing with its badge is centred, as the audit measured, and inside the live area.
- **Design source:** `visuals/src/r3/r3-art.json` now has the rescaled hourglass and the shipped `round`, so the N135
  test still compares the code with it.

**IL4 · accents**
- A badge (✓ or ∎ on a disc, `data-badge`) is always the brand indigo: it is a status mark. The subject colour stays
  on the object's own detail: the flag and waypoint, the star, the lens's handle, the sand.
- By day, topology and number theory take 20% ink (`color-mix`): they measured 2.72 and 2.86 : 1 on the disc. Every
  subject's accent and every badge now clears 3 : 1 in Day and Night.
