## N152 V20 motion in use: drag a sheet to dismiss, a count that counts (13 Nov 2026)

The audit's V20 found three motion helpers in `src/ui/v7/motion.ts` with no callers: `transitionPresets`, `dismissSheet`
and `interpolateNumber`. Two are now in use, and the third is gone.

**Drag a sheet down to dismiss** (`src/ui/v7/sheetDrag.ts`, `dismissSheet`)
- On a bottom sheet (phones, below 700 px), pressing the grabber or the header and pulling down moves the sheet with the
  finger. Buttons in the header are excluded.
- Letting go after 30% of the sheet's height, or with a downward flick faster than 600 px/s (measured over the last
  100 ms), closes it. It closes through the dialog's own `cancel` event, the path Escape takes, so the sheet's
  `onClose`, focus return and scroll unlock all run as before.
- Otherwise the sheet settles back in `--v7-m-quick` (instantly with reduced motion).
- The body does not drag, so lists inside a sheet scroll as before. A sheet shown as a centred dialog on wide screens
  does not drag.
- One delegated listener, installed once in `src/main.tsx`, serves every `dialog.v7-sheet`.
  `src/ui/v7/components.tsx` is unchanged, because another item owns it until its PRs merge. The listener could move into
  `Sheet` later.
- `sheet-drag.css` gives the grabber and header `touch-action: none` on phones, so the browser does not take the
  gesture.

**A number that animates** (`src/ui/v7/useAnimatedNumber.ts`, `interpolateNumber`)
- The Review hub's "cards today" counts to its new value in 240 ms (Motion v1 `base`, easing out) when the round
  changes: a subject in the legend, a deck switch, or a refresh. It passes through whole numbers.
- The first value after loading shows at once, so the count never counts up from the loading state.
- A change mid-count continues from the number on screen.
- With reduced motion or Reduce effects, `interpolateNumber(…, reduced)` jumps straight to the new value.

**Deleted:** `transitionPresets`, the kit's older `motion`-library presets. Motion v1 is written in CSS (N124), and
nothing used them. The existing tests of `dismissSheet` and `interpolateNumber` (`tests/unit/v7/haptics.test.ts`) are
unchanged.

**Checked:**
- `tests/unit/v7/n152-motion.test.tsx` covers:
  - the count-up: first value, after loading, mid-count change, reduced motion;
  - the drag: past 30%, a short slow pull, a fast flick, and the button, body and centred dialog that do not drag;
  - the release speed;
  - that both helpers are in use.
- `tests/e2e/v7-n152-motion.spec.ts`, at 390 px, checks that:
  - a 45% pull on the header closes "Customise this round" and returns focus to its button;
  - a 48 px slow pull follows the finger and settles back;
  - the hub's count passes through values between 23 and the subject's count, but only when motion is allowed.
