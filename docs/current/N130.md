## N130 v7 UX audit r3: the tracking checklist and the first fixes (12 Nov 2026)

The Owner asked to start on the v7 UX audit and plan r3 (`lemma-v7-ux-audit-and-plan-r3`, written on `d741617`) with a
checklist to collaborate on and track what is done.

**The plan in the repository.** `docs/v7/ux-audit-2026-11-12/` holds the plan's text unchanged (`00_UPDATE_R3.md` first,
then `01`–`06`), its 18 boards as 1600 px JPEGs, the measurements and re-checks, and the r3 proposal drawings
(`visuals/src/r3/r3-art.json`). **`CHECKLIST.md`** is the tracker: every finding (V, AW, IL, LT, IC), every phase item
(U0–U4, AN1–AN4, G0–G2, A5′), the Owner decisions E1–E23, the ten device checks and the definition of done, each with
its gate. A PR that finishes an item ticks it and adds its N-id and PR number.

**Fixed (U0 · Truth):**

- **V02 Today strip.** The hour labels were five labels spread edge to edge, so on an 08:00–22:00 window "14" sat over
  15:00 and the last label read "20" at 22:00. `stripTicks` (`src/features/home/v7/todayModel.ts`) now places each
  label at its own share of the window, on a whole-hour step that divides the window where one does (08:00–22:00 reads
  every two hours, the default 06:00–24:00 every three), with the window's end as the last label. The first and last
  labels align to the strip's edges. `stripHours` still returns the labels.
- **V03 One number, one value.** Practice read "0 of 1" on Today and "0 of 2" on Close the day, because Today counted
  only topics due exactly today. Both now use `practiceProgress` (`src/features/practice/progress.ts`): done = active
  topics practised that day in the owner's zone; due = active topics due that day or earlier and not practised. An
  overdue topic is still owed today. Today's "Needs you" rows are unchanged (overdue practice stays in the overdue
  count). The review estimate on Today ("about 9 min", 0.4 min a card) now comes from `useReviewEstimate`, the hook the
  Review hub uses ("About 10 min at your pace"); until it loads, Today shows the count without a time.
- **V17** Plan proposal: Reject on the left, Approve (still the wide primary) on the right, as in the Inbox.
- **V24** Quick focus: the built-in choice is "No timer · Count up from zero"; "Open" is left to open-mode presets.

**Fixed (G2 · the Lottie endings, P4 and P5):**

- **LT6** Round complete ended at half line weight: both cards listed their paper before their outline, and Lottie
  paints the first item on top. Strokes now come first in every group that has both.
- **LT4** The tray's and moon's fills fade in after their outlines are mostly drawn (frames 30–42 and 34–46), not with
  them.
- **LT5** The badge's tick starts 4 frames after its disc, not 12, so the disc never shows as a bare dot. The tick
  lands at frame 66 (Inbox zero), 72 (Round complete).
- **LT1** `LottieMoment` no longer shows the finished still and then erases it: while the player loads it shows only the
  backdrop (frame 1 of every animation, `IllustrationBackdrop`). If the player misses its 700 ms window or cannot load,
  the still fades in over 200 ms (`data-lottie-late`) and stays. Under reduced motion the still shows at once, as before.
- **LT2** The canvas has the screen's density (`canvasScale`: device pixel ratio between 1 and 3), not always 2×.
- **LT10** If Day/Night or contrast changes after an animation played, the player stops and the still, drawn from the
  live theme, replaces it.

Regenerate the animations with `node scripts/lottie/build.mjs`.
