## N208 Tutor scratch and photo answers

The opt-in `tutorScratch` flag adds drawings and camera or gallery photos to a learner answer. A compact scratch pad shares the chat surface. A finger or stylus draws, three fingers scroll the conversation and **Hand** pans the paper. The separator supports pointer and keyboard resizing. Undo and redo operate on strokes, including an entire eraser drag. The scratch pad menu includes pen width, accent, clearing, resizing and discarding. [Scratch pad references and tests](../ui-plan/2026-11-20/scratchpad-parity.md)

**Insert drawing** stages the cropped PNG and editable strokes in owner-scoped IndexedDB, then places an inline reference at the saved selection as one editor transaction. Failed image staging leaves the drawing ready to retry. Selecting an editable drawing reopens its strokes, and **Update drawing** replaces that occurrence in place. Removing and restoring a reference through text undo preserves its surrounding prose. Drawings and photos are capped at three attachments per answer. Local recovery restores references from earlier scratch drafts. [Inline editor and recovery tests](../../tests/e2e/v7-tutor-inline-editor.spec.ts), [Scratch pad browser tests](../../tests/e2e/v7-tutor-scratchpad.spec.ts)

**Send** follows the order of the references in the answer. The granted MCP read interleaves authorized image content with the learner’s prose. Images are re-encoded and bounded before upload, and the attachment migration binds the owner-folder objects to the learner turn in its transaction. Owner export includes image bytes. The attachment table cascades with its turn, and the cleanup worker purges expired unbound objects. The browser test mocks Supabase responses while it disconnects the thread, reloads a queued image-only answer from IndexedDB and verifies one upload per image and one turn submission on reconnect. A confirmed receipt clears only the submitted images and preserves newer draft edits. [Browser send and recovery tests](../../tests/e2e/v7-ui-plan-v2.spec.ts), [MCP image tests](../../tests/mcp/n208-tutor-images.test.ts)

An isolated Supabase Storage API v1.89.12 and PostgREST v14.5 on PostgreSQL 17 verified two signed test users, UUID paths, MIME and size limits, owner-only object access, duplicate upload lookup, atomic attachment binding, receipt replay and cleanup of expired unbound bytes. The migrations ran after the existing chain in that environment. Production verification follows migration review and deployment.
