## N153 V36 leftovers: the Inbox list's checkboxes and the card's review dots take a 44 px tap (13 Nov 2026)

Two controls still answered a tap in less than 44 px. The check: at 360 and 390 px, the element under the four points
21 px from the control's centre (up, down, left and right) must be the control or its label
(`tests/e2e/v7-n153-hit-areas.spec.ts`).

**Inbox list checkboxes** (`?view=list`)
- The box is 20 px and its label is 44 px tall. The row's 12 px leading padding belonged to the `li`, so a point 21 px
  left of the box's centre hit the row, not the label (all five rows failed left).
- Now the label carries that padding (`inbox.css`), so a tap up to 22 px left of the box ticks it. The row looks the same.

**The card's recall-chart review dots** (`/cards/:id`)
- Each review had a 44 px button centred on its dot. On a real card the reviews sit 0–7 px apart (the demo card has 18
  reviews, four within 6 px), so later buttons covered earlier ones. Some dots answered in as little as 28–34 px, and
  some points 21 px away chose another review.
- No layout can give each of four reviews 6 px apart its own 44 px target, so close reviews now share one.
  `tapGroups` (`recallModel.ts`) cuts the time axis midway between reviews, as early as it can while each target stays
  at least one touch target wide (`--v7-touch`: 44 px on the web, 48 px in the app).
- Each target spans its reviews' heights plus half a touch above and below. Targets sit side by side and never overlap,
  and every dot lies inside its own target.
- `RecallChart` measures its drawn width (ResizeObserver) to cut in real pixels. The demo card gets 5 targets at 360 px.
- A target holding one review reads as before ("Sun 18 Oct, Easy, next in 20 days"). A shared one reads "Wed 19 Aug to
  Thu 20 Aug, 2 reviews: Hard, Good".
- Tapping a shared target enlarges its dots and lists each review in the caption ("Wed 19 Aug · Hard · next in 6 min;
  Thu 20 Aug · Good · …").
- The History list below still has every review. The targets' corners are 4 px (were fully round), because Chromium
  hit-tests the rounded shape.
