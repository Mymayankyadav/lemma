## N228 Tutor quick questionnaires: short answer, one choice and several choices, kept until submitted

The Owner asked for a quick questionnaire the tutor attaches to its reply. It opens from the reply row, its icon colour shows whether it is unseen, started or complete, and the learner's answers are never lost.

- **Data** (`supabase/migrations/20261218002400_n228_tutor_questionnaires.sql`, additive only).
  - `tutor_questionnaires`: the tutor's questionnaire, attached to one turn.
    - A title, a purpose (`diagnose`, `check`, `practice`, `reflect` or `exit`) and 1 to 10 questions.
    - Each question has an id, a kind and a prompt. Kinds are `short` (no options), `mcq` (one of 2–8 options) and `msq` (any of 2–8 options).
    - The table checks the questions itself (`tutor_questionnaire_valid`).
  - `tutor_questionnaire_responses`: the learner's answers with a version, when they opened it, and when they submitted it. A submitted questionnaire stays submitted.
  - Both tables revoke direct writes from browsers, have the account-deletion guard and are in the owner export.
  - **Learner functions:** `tutor_questionnaire_state` (the thread's questionnaires and answers) and `tutor_questionnaire_save`.
    - `tutor_questionnaire_save` opens, saves or submits. It is version-checked, so a stale copy is not overwritten: the current one comes back as `conflict`. Answers must fit their questions, and submitting needs every question answered.
  - **Tutor function:** `tutor_questionnaire_connector` (service role only) authenticates exactly as the tutor connector does: the owner's connector secret and the handoff's grant.
    - `create` attaches a questionnaire only to a turn this grant answered. It is idempotent by id, and a reply carries at most two.
    - `list` returns the thread's latest ten with their answers.
- **MCP** (`supabase/functions/mcp/tutor-connector.ts`, `server.ts`).
  - The tutor attaches one with `study {resource:"tutor_questionnaires", operation:"create", target:{id:<handoff>}, operationId, patch:{title, purpose, questions}}`. The operationId is the questionnaire's id, so a retry is the same questionnaire.
  - It reads the answers with `get(what=tutor_questionnaires, id=<handoff>)`.
  - `supabase/functions/_shared/tutor-questionnaire.ts` holds the types and the same rules as the database, used by the MCP and the app.
- **The note to the tutor** (`QUESTIONNAIRE_NOTE`, sent with every handoff's instructions). It covers:
  - that the option exists, and when several short answers judge the learner better than one open question;
  - how to attach it, and the five purposes: diagnose before teaching, check what was just taught, practice earlier ideas, reflect on confidence, exit summary;
  - to say in the reply that one is attached;
  - how to read the answers and states, and to use them to judge the learner (the next question, what to reteach, save_progress), without grading unanswered questions.
- **App** (`TutorQuestionnaire.tsx`, `DemoCheckpointTutor.tsx`, `live-manual.ts`, `replyActions.css`).
  - **In the reply row:** each questionnaire is an icon after Make a card, labelled with its title and state. Its colour is the state: not opened is the selection colour with a dot, in progress is the warning colour, complete is the success colour.
  - **The sheet:** short answers are text boxes, one choice is radio buttons, several choices are checkboxes. Opening it marks it seen. Each change saves after a short pause, and the sheet says when it is saved.
  - **Until saved:** the answers are also kept on the device (`lemma.tutor.draft:[owner,path,checkpoint]:questionnaire:<id>`, under the tutor's account and deletion fence) and sent when saving works again.
  - **Saved on another device meanwhile:** the sheet restores this device's edited questions and cleared answers over the newer server version. It keeps each unsynced edit until the server acknowledges its value. If the other device already submitted, this sheet shows any unsent local edits with a status message and leaves the submission locked.
  - **Submit answers** is enabled once every question is answered. A submitted questionnaire is read-only.
  - The demo tutor has no questionnaires: they come only from a connected tutor.
- **Tests.**
  - `tests/mcp/sql/fixtures/n228-questionnaire-contract.mjs`, run by `tests/mcp/sql/n193-tutor.mjs`:
    - in PGlite and PostgreSQL: invalid questions, browser write privileges, open, partial saves, conflict, answer validation, submit rules, the submitted lock, and other users;
    - in PostgreSQL only: the connector's attach-after-reply, idempotent create, the limits and the tutor's list.
  - `tests/unit/mcp/tutor-questionnaire.test.ts`: the MCP attach and read, malformed questionnaires, error messages, and the note in every handoff.
  - `tests/unit/tutor/questionnaire-rules.test.ts`: the shared rules.
  - `tests/e2e/v7-tutor-questionnaire.spec.ts`: the reply-row icon and its colours, saving, a reload, offline answers kept on the device, an answer from another device, and submit.

## N228 additions (Owner, 23 Nov): post while the tutor has not replied, full-screen drawing with landscape, faster and more reliable ink

- **Posting while the tutor has not replied** (`supabase/migrations/20261218002400_…`, `tutor-connector.ts`, `DemoCheckpointTutor.tsx`).
  - **For the learner:** the Send button stays on while a turn waits. A message joins the waiting question (never a second pending turn), shows in the chat in order, and the draft clears. Up to 10 per question, text only: pictures wait for the reply and the composer says so. The placeholder reads "Add to your question…". If the tutor replied meanwhile, the server refuses and the message stays in the box, to be sent as the next answer. The label under the question says "Tutor has read your messages" once the tutor has them.
  - **Data:** `tutor_followups` (own id, turn, text, created, seen). Browsers cannot write it; it is in the owner export, has the account-deletion guard and a registry document. `tutor_followup_add` is idempotent by message id and takes the same lock as a reply, so a message and the reply that completes the turn are ordered. `tutor_followup_state` reads them. `tutor_followup_connector` (service role, connector secret and the turn's grant) reads them for the tutor and records when it has.
  - **MCP:** `get(what=tutor_turn)` and `get_tutor_turn` return `learnerFollowUps` with the turn. Reading and marking them seen uses the same checkpoint lock as posting and replying. The reply transaction refuses a new connector reply while any follow-ups are unseen. The tutor can read them and retry the same reply.
  - **Retries:** the composer keeps the message ID with its local draft. If a response is lost, the same send can recover the committed follow-up after reload or after the tutor replies.
- **Full screen, for both the scratch pad and equation mode** (`FullscreenPaper.tsx`, `keyboard.css`, `scratchpad.css`). A button at the paper's corner (in the pill for equation mode) opens it in the kit's modal Sheet over the whole screen: the page behind is inert and does not scroll, Tab stays inside, Escape or the same button leaves. The drawing state stays where it was, so the drawing is kept both ways. The paper keeps its own proportions (640 × 400) at one scale large enough to cover the screen, so what is drawn is what is sent; the larger side scrolls.
- **Landscape in full screen.** On a portrait screen a rotate button turns the whole surface a quarter one way, then the other, then back. The canvas maps every touch through its screen matrix (`getScreenCTM`) and a pan into the paper's own axes, so strokes are always stored upright in the paper's own frame; the equation reader takes the paper's layout size, not the turned bounding box. What is read or sent is therefore upright as it was drawn, with no turning afterwards. On a screen already in landscape the paper is upright and the button is not offered. The choice is kept while the app runs.
- **Ink speed and reliability** (`InkCanvas.tsx`, `ink.ts`). A stroke used to re-render the whole paper per move and was cut off silently after 256 points, a resting second finger cancelled it, and a cancelled pointer lost it.
  - The stroke being drawn is one path updated once per frame from every coalesced pointer event; committed strokes are drawn once (script time while drawing is about a third of before, measured with a 4× CPU slowdown).
  - A long stroke is simplified (Ramer–Douglas–Peucker, 0.6 paper units) and continues as another stroke if it must, so it is never cut off.
  - A second finger below the pan count (three on the scratch pad), or a palm within 0.8 s of the pen, no longer cancels or starts anything; a cancelled or lost pointer keeps what was drawn.
  - A full paper (96 strokes) says so when a stroke is started, instead of dropping it afterwards.
- **Tests.**
  - New: `v7-ink-drawing.spec.ts` (both papers; the long stroke, the second finger, a cancelled pointer, the palm, the full paper — 6 of the 8 fail on the old canvas), `v7-tutor-fullscreen-paper.spec.ts` (both papers, both orientations, drawing kept both ways, Escape, landscape both ways with upright strokes), `v7-tutor-followups.spec.ts`, `tests/unit/pad/ink-simplify.test.ts`, `ink-matrix.test.ts`, and follow-up cases in `tests/unit/mcp/tutor-questionnaire.test.ts` and `tests/mcp/sql/fixtures/n228-questionnaire-contract.mjs` (PGlite and PostgreSQL).
  - The live tutor specs' mocks answer the new read calls with an empty list (fixtures only). One assertion changes with the behaviour the Owner asked for: in `v7-tutor-chat.spec.ts` Send was expected to be disabled while a question waits for the tutor; it is now expected to be enabled (the message joins the waiting question, covered by `v7-tutor-followups.spec.ts`). The rest of that test is unchanged.
  - Review regressions cover concurrent follow-up posts and tutor reads or replies, reply errors, a lost acknowledgement after a committed follow-up, and stale questionnaire drafts with a newer server version.
