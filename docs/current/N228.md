## N228 Tutor quick questionnaires: short answer, one choice and several choices, kept until submitted

The Owner asked for a quick questionnaire the tutor attaches to its reply. It opens from the reply row, its icon colour shows whether it is unseen, started or complete, and the learner's answers are never lost.

- **Data** (`supabase/migrations/20261218002400_n228_tutor_questionnaires.sql`, additive only).
  - `tutor_questionnaires`: the tutor's questionnaire, attached to one turn.
    - A title, a purpose (`diagnose`, `check`, `practice`, `reflect` or `exit`) and 1 to 10 questions.
    - Each question has an id, a kind and a prompt. Kinds are `short` (no options), `mcq` (one of 2–8 options) and `msq` (any of 2–8 options).
    - The table checks the questions itself (`tutor_questionnaire_valid`).
  - `tutor_questionnaire_responses`: the learner's answers with a version, when they opened it, and when they submitted it. A submitted questionnaire stays submitted.
  - Both tables revoke direct writes from browsers, have the account-deletion guard and are in the owner export.
  - **Learner functions:** `tutor_questionnaire_state` (the thread's questionnaires and answers) and `tutor_questionnaire_save`.
    - `tutor_questionnaire_save` opens, saves or submits. It is version-checked, so a stale copy is not overwritten: the current one comes back as `conflict`. Answers must fit their questions, and submitting needs every question answered.
  - **Tutor function:** `tutor_questionnaire_connector` (service role only) authenticates exactly as the tutor connector does: the owner's connector secret and the handoff's grant.
    - `create` attaches a questionnaire only to a turn this grant answered. It is idempotent by id, and a reply carries at most two.
    - `list` returns the thread's latest ten with their answers.
  - The existing tutor functions are unchanged.
- **MCP** (`supabase/functions/mcp/tutor-connector.ts`, `server.ts`).
  - The tutor attaches one with `study {resource:"tutor_questionnaires", operation:"create", target:{id:<handoff>}, operationId, patch:{title, purpose, questions}}`. The operationId is the questionnaire's id, so a retry is the same questionnaire.
  - It reads the answers with `get(what=tutor_questionnaires, id=<handoff>)`.
  - `supabase/functions/_shared/tutor-questionnaire.ts` holds the types and the same rules as the database, used by the MCP and the app.
- **The note to the tutor** (`QUESTIONNAIRE_NOTE`, sent with every handoff's instructions). It covers:
  - that the option exists, and when several short answers judge the learner better than one open question;
  - how to attach it, and the five purposes: diagnose before teaching, check what was just taught, practice earlier ideas, reflect on confidence, exit summary;
  - to say in the reply that one is attached;
  - how to read the answers and states, and to use them to judge the learner (the next question, what to reteach, save_progress), without grading unanswered questions.
- **App** (`TutorQuestionnaire.tsx`, `DemoCheckpointTutor.tsx`, `live-manual.ts`, `replyActions.css`).
  - **In the reply row:** each questionnaire is an icon after Make a card, labelled with its title and state. Its colour is the state: not opened is the selection colour with a dot, in progress is the warning colour, complete is the success colour.
  - **The sheet:** short answers are text boxes, one choice is radio buttons, several choices are checkboxes. Opening it marks it seen. Each change saves after a short pause, and the sheet says when it is saved.
  - **Until saved:** the answers are also kept on the device (`lemma.tutor.draft:[owner,path,checkpoint]:questionnaire:<id>`, under the tutor's account and deletion fence) and sent when saving works again.
  - **Saved on another device meanwhile:** its answers are kept, with this device's changes on top.
  - **Submit answers** is enabled once every question is answered. A submitted questionnaire is read-only.
  - The demo tutor has no questionnaires: they come only from a connected tutor.
- **Tests.**
  - `tests/mcp/sql/fixtures/n228-questionnaire-contract.mjs`, run by `tests/mcp/sql/n193-tutor.mjs`:
    - in PGlite and PostgreSQL: invalid questions, browser write privileges, open, partial saves, conflict, answer validation, submit rules, the submitted lock, and other users;
    - in PostgreSQL only: the connector's attach-after-reply, idempotent create, the limits and the tutor's list.
  - `tests/unit/mcp/tutor-questionnaire.test.ts`: the MCP attach and read, malformed questionnaires, error messages, and the note in every handoff.
  - `tests/unit/tutor/questionnaire-rules.test.ts`: the shared rules.
  - `tests/e2e/v7-tutor-questionnaire.spec.ts`: the reply-row icon and its colours, saving, a reload, offline answers kept on the device, an answer from another device, and submit.

## Owner's follow-up: sending without the tutor, full-screen paper, reliable landscape ink

- The tutor's turn contract permits only one outstanding turn. A new answer composed while a reply is pending or the device is offline is **queued locally** in the scoped, persisted ink draft. The reply editor calls it "Queue answer", not "Send". It replays once the current turn is completed and a connection permits another submission. A queued item is **not** represented as delivered to the tutor until the server confirms it. It may be edited again with "Keep as draft". No database turn constraints were weakened.
- Both the Scratch pad and Equation mode have **Full screen** and **Landscape** controls. Full screen maximizes the paper and keeps Undo, recognition and insertion accessible. Landscape requests device orientation lock where supported; on portrait devices where that is unavailable, the editor rotates within the screen. Escape or the same control returns to the composer. The unfinished drawing remains in its existing durable draft.
- `InkCanvas` now paints the in-progress stroke directly on a single SVG path rather than re-rendering the entire drawing on every pointer movement. Coalesced pointer samples preserve handwriting curves. Pointer positions pass through the SVG's inverse screen transform, so a 90-degree rotated paper is saved and fed to on-device recognition in the normal upright paper coordinates instead of sideways.
- `tests/unit/tutor/fullscreen-ink.test.ts` covers inverse coordinate mapping under quarter-turn rotation, scaling, and singular transforms. Browser/mobile handwriting and orientation lock require a real-device smoke check; CSS fallback is available where orientation lock is denied.
