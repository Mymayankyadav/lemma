## N204 Adaptive information placement

The Atlas, Inbox, and checkpoint panels use separate feature components. Inbox keeps one row per suggested link and groups batch decisions under Review links by kind. Calendar draws placement suggestions in the grid. Settings → Advanced contains model health, track record, calibration, knowledge by area, diagnostic keys and decision details. Path shows the remaining-work estimate beside its progress, with full estimates in a disclosure.

Study details retain scheduler fit, budgets, practice estimates and queue rationale. Session attempt history and checkpoint readiness are available on demand. Atlas Layers retains study-state subsets and subject navigation; themes and bridges retain proficiency/strength details. These are reorganized controls, not deleted capabilities.
