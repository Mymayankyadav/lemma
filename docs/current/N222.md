## N222 Equation mode with auto-equation (22 Nov 2026)

The Owner asked for an "auto eq" model that turns *root 2* into √2, and an equation-writing mode that applies it while an equation is written. Later the Owner asked that an ambiguous equation such as `x2+y2=1` offer both its readings (a quadratic or a linear equation), and that the keyboard's layout not change.

- **Turning it on.** √ in the answer keyboard's strip turns equation mode on (shown pressed) and off. The keyboard, its strip and its keys keep their positions and sizes, which an e2e test checks.
- **What converts.** In equation mode, `src/features/tutor/input/autoEquation.ts` converts each piece of typed maths as soon as it is finished, the way Gboard autocorrects a word.
  - It acts only in prose: inside `$…$` the learner writes LaTeX, where `x^2` is already right.
  - It makes one change per keystroke, from the one place where typing is committed. That covers the answer keyboard and a hardware keyboard.
  - **Roots:** `root 2`, `sqrt 2`, `sqrt(2)`, `square root of x` → √2, √x; `sqrt(x+1)` → √(x+1); `cube root 8` → ∛8; `fourth root of x` → ∜x.
  - **Powers and indices:** `x^2`, `x^{-1}`, `(a+b)^n` → x², x⁻¹, (a+b)ⁿ; `x squared` → x², `cubed` → ³; `a_n`, `x_{12}` → aₙ, x₁₂.
  - **Operators, once finished:** `->` `<-` `<->` `=>` `<=>` `<=` `>=` `!=` `~~` `==` `+-` `|->` `...`, and `2*3` → 2·3.
  - **Fractions:** `1/2` `1/3` `2/3` `1/4` `3/4` `1/8`.
  - **Written-out names:** Greek letters (ε and φ upright), `in` ∈, `to` →, `infinity` ∞, `sum` ∑, `int` ∫, `partial` ∂, `forall` ∀, `exists` ∃, `union` ∪, `subseteq` ⊆, `empty` ∅, `RR` ℝ, and the like.
- **Ambiguous equations.** A variable with a digit (`x2`) is never changed by itself. The strip offers both readings of the equation being typed, which starts after the last prose word on the line:
  - `x2+y2=1` offers **x²+y²=1** and x₂+y₂=1.
  - The quadratic reading comes first when every digit is 2 or 3 and nothing is listed with commas.
  - Otherwise the indices come first: `x1 + x2 = 1` and `a1, a2, a3`.
  - Function names (sin, log, lim…) count as part of the equation.
- **Reversible.** A Backspace straight after a change puts the typed text back, with its separator, as after a Gboard autocorrection. So does **Keep** in the strip's left slot, and Undo typing in ⋯.
- **Equation strip.** In equation mode the word aid (prose completions and spellings) steps aside. The three slots show, in this order of priority:
  1. both readings of an ambiguous equation, with the ellipsis;
  2. Keep after a change;
  3. the usual next pieces: after a variable, its square and indices (`y` → y₁ · y² · yₙ); after a number or bracket, an exponent (⁻¹ · ² · ³); after a comma, the ellipsis; otherwise the question's own variables (the single letters of the tutor's question), 0 and ….
