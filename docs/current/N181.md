## N181 Atlas v3 attempts follow their session event

`supabase/migrations/20261218000700_n181_atlas_attempts_follow_event.sql` adds `atlas_attempts_follow_event()` and the trigger `atlas_attempts_follow` on `session_events`, beside the N169 `lemma_evidence_follow`:
- **Void** (`void_session_event`): the attempts written from the event and its instruction events (`source_key event:<id>:…`) are voided.
- **Correct** (`correct_session_event`): each live attempt is voided and superseded by a new attempt on the corrected event. If the outcome and hint level are unchanged (wording, kind or time fixed), the new attempt is an exact copy with its stages and tags. If they changed, it carries the corrected outcome as `legacy_outcome`, one tag per concept and facet (strongest role), no stage, the corrected hint level, and leaves out concepts the attempt never reached. A correction to an ungraded kind leaves no attempt.
- Every write lands in the correction's action, so `undo` of the correction or the void restores the attempts exactly (the usual rule applies: undo a later action that touched the same rows first).
- Before the cutover (no attempts) and after the N172 rollback (no `attempts` table) the trigger does nothing.

Why it matters: without it, a corrected or voided event after the cutover would keep feeding the model through its old attempt, since the model reads attempts, not evidence. Production has no attempts yet, so deploying it changes nothing until activation.
