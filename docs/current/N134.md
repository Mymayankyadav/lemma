## N134 v7 UX audit r3: large text holds (U2 · V11) (13 Nov 2026)

People who raise Android's text size saw the v7 screens collide at 360 px (the audit's V11, measured with the page's
root font at 130%). The web layer now holds at 130% and 200%; `tests/e2e/v7-n134-text-scale.spec.ts` checks the
screens below at 360 px with both sizes, and 390 px at 100% to guard the default look.

- **Dock.** `useTabLabelsFit` (`src/app/v7/V7Shell.tsx`) measures whether every tab name fits its slot. When one does
  not (large text, a narrow phone, a longer language), the dock shows the icons and only the selected tab's name; the
  others stay in the accessibility tree. Names never wrap. At 390 px with normal text all five names show, as before.
- **Calendar.** The Day and Week hour gutters grow with the text (`max(44px, 2.2rem + 8px)`, `max(36px, 1.9rem + 4px)`),
  so "12:00" keeps its first digit; at 100% they are 44 and 36 px as before.
- **Segmented controls** (`Segmented`, `useSegmentsFit` in `src/ui/v7/components.tsx`). They measured nothing, so labels
  overlapped ("Time blocCard") or wrapped inside a segment. Now a label never wraps by accident; when the labels do not
  fit, a choice of more than three becomes the kit's Picker menu and up to three wrap onto two lines on purpose. A hidden
  copy keeps measuring, so the segments come back when there is room (it is clipped, so it never widens the page: the
  existing 200%-at-390 px check caught 7 px of sideways scroll on Progress before it was). Capture's type is a menu at 360 px (where
  "Time block" overruns its segment even at 100%) and segments at 390 px.
- **Focus.** One column as wide as the screen: the header, the clock (capped at 21vw) and the controls row (which now
  wraps) no longer run past the edge at 200%; the thought field and Drifted share a row only while both fit.
- **Stat tiles** (Review, round complete, Progress, Card): three across at 320–390 px with normal text, two or one as
  the text grows (`auto-fit`, at least 5.5rem each).

Still open in U2 (see `CHECKLIST.md`): the design lint's own "360 at 130%" pass over every scene, the six controls under
44 px (V36), the first-load budget (V21, E12) and honouring the system text size in the app (AW1, E5), which can follow
now that the layouts hold.
