## N129 A3 Lottie moments: Inbox zero, Round complete, Day closed (12 Nov 2026)

The Owner approved the dotLottie web player (`@lottiefiles/dotlottie-web` 0.81.0) as the A3 dependency, knowing its
real size: about 15 KB gzipped of JavaScript plus a 1.2 MB WebAssembly renderer (about 505 KB gzipped).

What exists now:

- **Three animations, drawn in code** by `scripts/lottie/build.mjs` (no design tool, no marketplace files) in the A2
  house style, written to `src/ui/v7/lottie/*.json` (about 4 KB each):
  - L-11 Inbox zero: the tray draws itself, then a ✓ badge grows. Still: IL-04 `inbox`.
  - L-10 Round complete: a card settles onto the deck, its lines are written, then the ✓ badge. Still: IL-03 `review`.
  - L-12 Day closed: a crescent moon is drawn, two stars come out, then ∎. Still: the new `closed` illustration,
    which replaces the bare ∎ on the Day closed screen.
  Each plays once at 60 fps in at most 1.6 s, eases out without overshoot ("nothing bounces"), starts on the backdrop
  (a calm frame 1) and ends exactly on its still.
- **Theming:** every fill and stroke is named after a role (`bg`, `ink`, `muted`, `paper`, `accent`, `onAccent`);
  `themeLottie` (`src/ui/v7/lottie.ts`) fills them in from the page's v7 roles, so Day, Night and the subject colour
  follow. This replaces the plan's `.lottie` theme slots with a simpler equivalent.
- **Loading:** `LottieMoment` shows the A2 still at once. When motion is allowed it imports `lottiePlayer.ts` (the
  player, the WebAssembly URL and the animation) and draws the animation over the still. The WebAssembly is served
  from Lemma's own build (`DotLottie.setWasmUrl`), never from the player's default CDN; it is not precached by the
  service worker (only `js,css,html,png,svg,woff2` are), so installs do not grow.
- **When it does not play:** under reduced motion (system or Settings › Reduce effects) the player is never fetched.
  It also does not play offline, on any load error, or when the player is not ready within 700 ms; in each case the
  still stays.
- **Day closed** now waits 2.6 s, not 1.6 s, before returning home when motion is allowed, so the animation finishes.
  It still waits 0.4 s under reduced motion.
- **Round paused** and the loading state keep the old ✓ mark. Only a finished round gets the animation.

Known limits and later work:

- On a first web visit over a slow network the WebAssembly can take longer than 700 ms. The still then shows, and later
  moments play from the HTTP cache. The Android app serves it locally.
