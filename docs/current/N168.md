## N168 Calendar: a saved timer session is one card you can tap (Owner task, 2026-11-16)

What changed, from the Owner's screenshot of Calendar Day (two cut-off green strips, and nothing happened on tap):

- **One card per saved session.** Day, Week and Agenda draw each saved, not voided study entry as one card from its first
  focus to its last (`src/features/calendar/v7/loggedSessions.ts`). Before, Day drew one strip per focus interval, so every
  pause split a session into separate events, and Agenda listed one row per slice.
- **Pauses and breaks are drawn inside the card.** Pausing closes the focus interval and records nothing until Resume
  opens a new one, so a pause is the gap no interval covers: drawn hatched. A gap covered by a `break` interval is a break,
  drawn dotted. The caption reads `35 min · paused 15 min` (plus `· break 5 min` when there was one). Only focus is
  counted, as before; Today, Progress and the Week strip totals (`savedSlices`, `dayCapacity`) are unchanged.
- **Tap opens the session.** Logged cards are buttons (`data-logged-entry`). The new `LoggedSessionSheet` shows the day and
  span, the path, Focus / Paused / Break totals, each stretch at its times, rating, reflection and source. **Edit times** opens
  the existing time-log editor (`EntrySheetV7`: intervals, split, path); **Void** voids the entry with Undo (restore). The
  open sheet rides in the URL as `logged=<entry id>`, so Back closes it.
- **Short cards are never cut through.** A card under 46 px (about 43 minutes in Day) is one centred row: the title with an
  ellipsis, then the caption; under 26 px the row is set in the caption size (`data-compact`, `data-tiny` on
  `.v7-cal-block`). This applies to planned blocks too; before, a title and caption that did not fit were both clipped mid-line.

Known, not changed here: `v7-part4` "Calendar Day moves by week … back to today" fails on days when the real today is in the
start week (issue #1389).

Tests: `tests/unit/v7/n168-logged-sessions.test.tsx`.
