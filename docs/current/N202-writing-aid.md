## N202 local writing aid and physical-device preview

The owner continues the existing writing-aid work in PR #1738. This revision replaces swipe acceptance with tap acceptance, adds theme-coded suggestion categories, and evaluates structured tutor signals and personal automatic corrections. It does not authorize production merge.

The composer strikes through a proposed source and floats a compact tap-to-accept card above the input. Refresh and X remain next to the suggestion; the keyboard refreshes three stationary word targets. Desktop Tab accepts a faded continuation. Swipes retain native selection and scrolling. Amber spelling, blue notation, green continuation and purple word tokens have labels/icons, with Day/Night contrast and touch-target checks. Accepted changes have nearby Undo.

Both tutor reply transports accept optional bounded writingAid metadata: exact visible fragments, roles, variables and symbols. It travels through the existing owner-scoped reply body without a database migration. The app uses only the current connector reply, binds its source ID, hides validated metadata, and invalidates stale offers when the content changes. Manual imports do not supply writing signals. Independent learner-prefix and content guards reject unchosen proof methods, operands, polarity and variables; a tutor role label cannot override those checks. Strongly typed lexical endings and neutral exact-source wording remain eligible. Accepted text cannot become new learner evidence.

Settings → Tutor → Writing aid and chat → Writing assistance share owner-scoped modes and surface preferences. The new automatic-correction switch permits four surface typo pairs only after two explicit manual acceptances of the exact pair, without intervening negative feedback, and a qualifying synthetic calibration certificate. Corrections occur on direct word separators and have immediate Undo/Backspace recovery. Rejection revokes permission and suppresses that occurrence. Permission expires after thirty days. Unconditional cold-start corrections failed calibration and remain manual. These confidence estimates describe simulated profiles, not measured human precision.

Tutor hints defaults to Wording, the provisional validation winner. Wording and notation enables the broader experimental signals; Off supplies a no-context comparison. The sealed test did not show an incremental WPM benefit over the previous contextual ranker. A subsequent Faster-mode exposure finding was fixed and replayed as a regression, with the original result preserved.

The bounded local reward learner has ten original feedback channels plus separate automatic-retention and automatic-Undo rewards. Automatic application does not count as manual acceptance. Off stops assistance/collection; personalization can be paused or forgotten. Demo defaults to enabled assistance, while live profiles retain Off. The automatic policy still requires the two manual choices in demo.

See [current design](../implementation/2026-11-21/writing-aid-tutor/DESIGN.md) and the candidate comparison, datasets, fitted weights and validation in that directory. The earlier adaptive and fixed-row reports remain historical baselines. The side-by-side debug APK is for the owner's physical trial. Ready and production deployment remain withheld pending that trial and the owner's model decision.
