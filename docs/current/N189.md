Baseline: `38ee9be32fbc250a05b529d7dd2662e7ed4e3c1f`, the 20 November plan's actual starting checkout, not its historical UI-audit revision. This is a scoped P0/P1 implementation, not a claim that all first-use, receipt, or release gates have passed.

**Capture.** Quick and expanded task entry share typed form values and the zone used to parse them. Time-only uses the owner's civil day, date-only stays civil, and no due value stays unplanned. DST gaps fail visibly; editing an existing second-fold instant without changing its clock keeps its instant. A block handed to the full form retains Study/Busy, exact bounds, links and text without putting the draft in the URL.

**Review.** The former card-detail link opened a path; the live start RPC accepted no concrete card and picked its own first eligible item. The new optional `filters.cardId` selects the requested owner-visible active card before sibling ranking, subject to the same owner-day eligibility and remaining review/new quotas. It either snapshots one versioned card or fails without starting a round; it cannot substitute another. General rounds still use their original selector. Both clients reopen the persisted run ID and resolve its card ID directly rather than looking it up in the general quota-limited list. The change does not add grants, change authentication or alter FSRS grading.
