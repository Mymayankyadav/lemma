## N252 iPad: the tutor's keyboard follows iPadOS, and headers get iPadOS's spacing

Owner, with screenshots of the tutor and of the native keyboard on their iPad: "This is how it looks on my ipad. And this how the native keyboard looks on iPad. I understand that keyboard layout should depend on the device. Keep the current layout for android devices."

Owner follow-up, with a screenshot of Today on the iPad: "Also for iPad you might have to add proper padding in the header. You can see earlier screenshot of Claude app to see the proper padding"

### Keyboard

**Before.** Every device drew the same Gboard phone layout (N220/N221). On an iPad its ten keys per row were stretched across the composer: caps about twice as wide as tall, 48 px rows, digit hints in the corners, Enter as a pill in the bottom row.

**Which layout.** `src/features/tutor/keyboardLayout.ts` decides:
- `ipad` on an iPad in a window at least 600 px wide. An iPad is an `iPad` user agent, or desktop-mode Safari: a `Mac` platform with more than one touch point. The test lives in `src/platform/ipad.ts` and is shared with the paper's Pencil-only setting.
- `gboard` everywhere else: the Android app, Android browsers, iPhone, desktops, and an iPad in Slide Over or a narrow Split View, where iPadOS itself switches to its phone keyboard.

`useKeyboardLayout()` follows the window width as it changes. The composer passes the layout to `TutorKeyboard`. On an iPad it also marks the keyboard `data-keyboard-layout="ipad"` and drops the strip's Hide keyboard button, because the keys have their own.

**The iPad layout** follows iPadOS's full-size keyboard, with key widths measured on the Owner's screenshot of it. Every row is 12.5 letter keys across, so the letters are staggered as on the iPad:

| Row | Keys (widths in letter keys) |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⇥ tab 1.25 · q–p · ⌫ delete 1.25 |
| 2 | ⇪ caps lock 1.55 · a–l · ↵ return 1.95 |
| 3 | ⇧ shift 2 · z–m · `!,` · `?.` · ⇧ shift 1.5 |
| 4 | maths ∀∃ 1.05 · .?123 1.05 · space 7.4 · .?123 1.5 · hide keyboard 1.5 |

- **Maths key.** It sits where iPadOS puts its emoji key. There is no dictation key, so the space bar takes that room.
- **Modifier keys.** They show iPadOS's symbols, not words, in the outer bottom corner: tab, caps lock, the left shift and the maths key at the bottom left; delete, return, the right shift, the right .?123 and hide at the bottom right; the left .?123 at the bottom centre. The accessible names are Tab, Delete, Caps lock, Return, Shift and Hide keyboard. The keys are in the tinted modifier shade. A lit shift or caps lock turns light with a filled, selection-coloured arrow. Shift still locks with a quick double tap.
- **Secondary characters.** They stand grey above their letters: 1–0, then `@ # $ & * ( ) ' "`, then `% - + = / ; :`, and `!` and `?` on the punctuation keys. `$` stands where iPadOS shows the local currency, because `$` delimits maths in an answer. Holding a key for 380 ms types its secondary character. iPadOS uses a downward flick, but a flick here would draw a symbol over the keys (N249).
- **No enlarged preview** above a pressed key, as on iPadOS.
- **Tab** does what Tab does on a hardware keyboard in the composer: it takes the shown continuation, or goes to the next □. Otherwise it types a tab character.
- **Return** types a new line, the same as Gboard's Enter.
- **Number pages** keep Lemma's symbols in the same frame:
  - .?123: tab · 1–0 · delete; `+ − × ÷ = < > ( ) /` · return; #+= · `^ _ ' : ; ! ? , .` · #+=.
  - #+=: tab · `² ³ ⁿ ⁻¹ ₀ ₁ ₙ ° ′ ″` · delete; `[ ] { } | \ % # & @` · return; 123 · `· … ∘ ≈ ± ∞ ~ * $` · 123.
  - The bottom row shows ABC in place of .?123.
- **Maths category pages** are unchanged, with a hide key added beside Delete.
- **Size.** Caps are 10 px apart, as on iPadOS. Rows are `clamp(44px, 7dvh, 60px)` high: about 50 px caps in portrait and 53 px rows in landscape. The text and symbol sizes scale with the row height, so the grey characters stay clear of their letters in landscape. Short landscape windows (under 560 px high) keep the compact landscape rule.

**Word decoder.** The decoder's touch model (`ngramSpatial`) places its key centres in Gboard's frame, where A is half a key and Z a key and a half past Q. On the iPad they are 0.3 and 0.75 of a key past Q. An iPad tap is now measured from the first letter of its row and moved into Gboard's frame, so the decoder's spatial scores hold on either layout. Tap correction uses the keys' real boxes and needed no change.

### Headers

**Before.** The installed web app does not ask for a translucent status bar, so iPadOS shows the page below its status bar and reports no top safe area. Today's header then sat 8 px down in a 56 px row, which centred it 36 px under the status bar. The Claude app centres its bar about 22 pt under the status bar. The day's large title sat 8 px below the row, so it looked pressed against the header.

**Now.** `main.tsx` marks the document `data-ipad` before the first paint (`markIPad`, `src/platform/ipad.ts`; not in the Android app). On an iPad:
- Today's header row is 48 px tall and 4 px from the top of the page, or from the safe area when there is one. That centres it 28 px under the status bar, where the other hubs' bars already are.
- The day's title has 24 px of room above it instead of 8.
- The rail's + stays centred on the header row.

Other tablets, desktops and phones keep the header as it was.

### Unchanged

Gboard's layout, geometry and behaviour on every other device (the Gboard layout specs pass as before), the strip, equation mode, Draw symbol, key ink, and the headers on every device but the iPad.

### Tests

`tests/unit/tutor/ipad-keyboard.test.tsx` covers:
- which devices get which layout, and that only an iPad is marked `data-ipad`;
- the iPad rows and their 12.5-key widths;
- the modifier symbols and the secondary characters;
- typing, holding for a secondary character, shift, caps lock, tab, return, delete and hide;
- the number pages;
- the decoder frame (it fails without the conversion);
- Gboard's rows unchanged.

`tests/e2e/v7-tutor-ipad-keyboard.spec.ts` emulates an iPad in portrait and landscape and checks:
- the keys, the stagger, near-square caps, and grey characters clear of their letters;
- typing `Hi, @XYz` with shift, hold and caps lock;
- the number pages;
- Tab to the next □;
- hiding the keyboard;
- that Android, iPhone and a narrow iPad window keep Gboard's layout.

`tests/e2e/v7-ipad-header.spec.ts` checks:
- on an iPad, Today's header row position and height, the title's room and the rail's + alignment, and that the other hubs' bars match;
- on other devices, Today's header as it was.
