## N209 Detail pages and one bottom bar

The immersive route inventory controls whether the tab bar mounts. Detail pages use the shared dock for their actions. Tutor Back restores the originating detail page, with the checkpoint as its direct-link fallback. Offline status remains in the page flow and marks answers and approvals as unsaved. Cards and Tasks have filter sheets and the tab bar contains the phone focus retainer.
