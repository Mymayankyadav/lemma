## N151 Offline made visible: "as of HH:MM" on Today and the Review hub (13 Nov 2026)

AN3 of the v7 UX audit r3 asks for "as of 14:05" when Today's and the Review hub's values come from the device rather than
the server. The header ℓ as a sync light is left for later.

**What counts as the saved copy** (`src/ui/v7/savedCopy.ts`, `savedCopyAt`). A value shown on screen comes from the saved
copy when:
- the browser is offline (its `online`/`offline` events, read with `useOnline`);
- a refresh is paused waiting for the network (TanStack `fetchStatus: 'paused'`); or
- the latest refresh failed while the older value stays on screen (`isError` with data).

The chip shows the time of the oldest such value (`dataUpdatedAt`). A source with nothing on screen is not a saved copy.
`src/lib/offline-cache.ts` (a snapshot kept across launches) is still not wired. Until it is, the saved copy is what this
tab already loaded.

**The chip** (`src/ui/v7/AsOf.tsx`). It reuses the kit's meta chip, with a new `saved` kind and the History icon. It reads
"as of 14:05" in the learner's time zone. It is a `role="status"`, and screen readers hear "Working from your notebook ·
as of 14:05" (the web plan's wording). It renders nothing while the numbers are fresh.

**Today** (`V7Today.tsx`). The chip sits at the end of the briefing. Its sources are:
- `home`;
- `settings`;
- the day's own data (blocks, entries, tasks, practice, answered), now stamped with the time it loaded.

Two behaviour changes:
- When the day's data cannot load and nothing is on screen yet, Today shows the last day it loaded in this tab (same day
  and time zone), with the chip, instead of "Couldn't load Today". Before, a return to Today while offline lost it.
- Coming back online reloads the day, so the chip goes.

**Review hub** (`ReviewHubV7.tsx`). The chip sits at the end of the note under the count. Its sources are decks, review
items, settings, home, insights and the forecast. Two behaviour changes:
- A failed refresh of decks, items or settings shows the error only when there is nothing to show. With older numbers in
  hand, the hub keeps them, with the chip. Before, any failed refresh replaced the hub with "Could not load your review
  decks".
- The forecast chart does the same with "Could not load the forecast".
