## N164.2 — the other six reminder kinds reach the phone

`supabase/functions/push/notice.ts` now maps every kind of `notify_candidates` to the existing rules engine and copy, with a stable key and a link into the app. Defaults are the ones Settings › Reminders shows (study time 09:00, at most 5 a day, quiet 22:00–07:00, digest on Sunday at 09:00), so the server does what the screen says.

| Kind | When | Key (one row per owner and key) | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| due | once a day at the study time, at least the threshold due, nothing studied yet; needs `kinds.due` | `due:<day>` | Review |
| deadline | tomorrow's checkpoints, once a day at the study time | `deadline:<day>` | the Path (Calendar for several) |
| behind | each checkpoint three days past its date, repeated after 7 days (14 after three dismissals), one project a day | `behind:<slug>:<code>` | the Path |
| resume | a session paused 18 hours, never after studying today, at most twice | `resume:<slug>:<session>` | the Path |
| tutor | fresh proposals once settled, or a saved session summary, two hours apart | `tutor:<newest>` | Inbox |
| digest | weekly on the owner's day and time (daily summary in daily mode) | `digest:weekly:<day>` / `digest:daily:<day>` | Progress / Today |
| plan | a plan now ends after its project's target date | `plan:<slug>:<target>:<plan end>` | the Path |
| rhythm | after two quiet days with cards due and a checkpoint to name; needs `kinds.rhythm` | `rhythm:<day>` | Review |

Only a decision to *send* is queued, and a key already used is not queued again (event keys that were already sent are not even offered to the rules, so a duplicate cannot take a tick's one send). The rules' own limits apply unchanged: one send per tick, an hour apart, the daily cap, quiet hours, a live session. `tests/unit/push/notice.test.ts` checks every kind at its boundaries.
