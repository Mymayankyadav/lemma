## N164.3 — buttons, Web Push, Settings, and area 30 closed

- **Buttons.** A notice carries up to two buttons (`ActionSpec`): a link opens the app; every other action carries a token made at delivery with the existing `notify/action-token.ts` (HMAC with the function's `PUSH_SHARED_SECRET`, naming the owner, the notification and the one action, 47 hours). Later and Not today (due, deadline, behind, resume, rhythm, tutor), Approve N cards (tutor, only with the owner's "New card proposals" opt-in, only cards without a plan, at most 20 at a time), Undo (the confirmation). A button that cannot be signed is left out, never sent unsigned.
- **The route.** The phone's receiver (`NoticeActionReceiver`) posts `{"act":{"token"}}` to the `push` function. The token is the only credential (the function is deployed without the gateway's JWT check, like the timer route): `handleAction` verifies it, calls `notify_act` (each notification is used once; a repeat answers `already_done`) and dismisses the notification only on HTTP 200, so a tap made offline can be made again. After an approval the confirmation `notify_act` writes is sent to the phone with an Undo button (`confirm`).
- **Web Push.** An owner with no phone but a browser subscription gets the same notice by Web Push when the function has `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` and `VAPID_SUBJECT`; the result is recorded by `notify_web_record` (success clears the failure count, a gone endpoint is disabled, a third failure disables it). An owner with both gets only FCM. Without the VAPID secrets such an owner is not delivered to (`no_device`).
- **Settings.** On a phone registered for push, Settings › Reminders has a switch for every kind (like the web) and a "Send a test reminder" row (`request_notify_test`: one test row queued for the signed-in owner, at most one a minute, delivered at once). Without push the app keeps the earlier list ("In your Inbox on this phone").
- **Area 30** of `docs/mcp/capability-map.json` is `supported` and the N160 item leaves NEXT.md with it.

**Owner setup.** Nothing new is needed for FCM. For Web Push only, add the three VAPID secrets to the function. Reminders stay off until the owner switches them on in Settings.
