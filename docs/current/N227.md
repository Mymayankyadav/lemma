## N227 Equation mode's floating tools and finger scrolling, the strip pencil opens the scratch pad, quieter chat notices, and Atlas typed links

The Owner reviewed equation mode, the pencil and the Atlas on the phone (23 Nov) and asked for five changes.

- **Equation mode** (`TutorEquationPanel.tsx`, `keyboard.css`).
  - **Floating tools.** The row holding Read, Undo and Clear is gone, so the paper takes its place. The three tools float as a pill in the paper's top-right corner. While a stroke is being drawn they fade out and stop taking touches (`data-hidden`), so they never cover the pen.
  - **Resize grip.** A grip on the paper's top edge drags it taller, by up to 32% of the window, the same way the scratch pad's grip works. It is a `separator` labelled "Resize writing paper"; ArrowUp and ArrowDown change it in steps of 32 px. The height is kept while the app runs.
  - **Finger scrolling.** Two fingers scroll the paper both ways. Three fingers scroll the chat (the conversation's `[role="log"]`). `InkCanvas`'s `onPan` now reports how many fingers moved, and the drawing pen still uses one finger.
- **Pencil** (`TutorComposer.tsx`).
  - With ink on, the pencil at the strip's left edge opens the scratch pad ("Scratch"). It no longer opens Draw symbol's Detexify search.
  - The pencil in the input box is removed; with ink on, the box has no left button.
  - When the answer has an unfinished slot, Next slot (⇒) moves from the strip's left edge to the end of the quick slots.
  - Draw symbol (Detexify) is still reached on the plain-answer path (`-tutorScratch`). With ink on, single symbols are still read automatically in equation mode.
- **Unfinished drawing.** The sentence "Insert or discard your unfinished drawing before sending." read like a tutor question. It is now a small pill, "Unfinished drawing · Open", that reopens the scratch pad. Sending is still blocked until the drawing is inserted or discarded.
- **Make a card** (`DemoCheckpointTutor.tsx`, `tutor.css`).
  - Under a tutor reply, "Make a card…" is replaced by a card icon (`SquarePlus`, "Make a card") that opens a **Make a card** sheet with Front, Back and Propose card. The sheet closes once the card is proposed.
  - The icon sits in a "Reply actions" row together with the tutor's card suggestions, which show as compact chips with Review proposal or their status. The row has room for later chat-specific actions, such as opening the cards the tutor suggested or a quick questionnaire.
- **Atlas typed links** (`typedLinks.ts`, `scene.ts`, `AtlasMap.tsx`, `AtlasV7.tsx`, `AtlasLayers.tsx`, `atlas.css`).
  - The Layers sheet (the Atlas's filter menu) gains a **Typed links** switch, showing a count and turned off by default. It is disabled when no concepts on the map are linked.
  - When it is on, the map draws Atlas V3's links between concepts as curved arcs in a colour and dash per kind:
    - Equivalent and Analogous: the selection colour.
    - Generalises and Instance of: success.
    - Uses technique: warning.
    - Contrasts with: danger.
    - Part of: secondary text.
  - Kinds that share a colour have different dashes, so the kinds can be told apart without colour.
  - Proposed links are drawn fainter than approved ones. Rejected links, self-links and links to concepts not on the map are left out.
  - A key in the sheet lists each kind with its line and count.
  - The choice is kept on the device (`lemma.atlas.typed-links`).
- **Tests.**
  - New: `tests/unit/atlas/typed-links.test.ts` and `tests/e2e/v7-atlas-typed-links.spec.ts`.
  - `v7-tutor-inline-math.spec.ts` gains tests for the strip pencil and the empty input box, the floating tools hiding during a stroke, the grip, two-finger scrolling of the paper and three-finger scrolling of the chat.
  - Changed call sites only, no assertion removed or loosened:
    - The scratch pad is opened through the strip's Scratch button with `tests/e2e/helpers/scratch.ts`.
    - The live tutor spec makes a card through the Make a card icon and sheet.
    - Feature parity measures the strip's Scratch button in place of the input box's pencil.
- **Not yet checked on a phone.** Two- and three-finger scrolling is tested in Chromium with CDP touch events. It has not been tried on the Owner's phone.
