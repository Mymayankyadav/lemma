**AW6 · the haptic vocabulary (E17)** (`src/ui/v7/haptics.ts`)

| Moment | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Reveal (Show answer, Space, a swipe) | `reveal`, one soft tick (5 ms) |
| Good, Hard, Easy | `grade`, one click |
| Again | `gradeAgain`, two light ticks |
| Task tick (Tasks, as the tick finishes drawing; Today's checkbox) | `check`, a click |
| Ending (Inbox zero, Round complete, Day closed) | `complete` (LT8) |

**LT7 · Lottie guard rails (E22)** (`lottie.ts`, `LottieMoment.tsx`, `lottiePlayer.ts`)
- **Skipped:** with `navigator.connection.saveData`, or an `effectiveType` of `2g` or `slow-2g`, the player is never
  fetched and the still shows, as under reduced motion.
- **Warmed:** `warmMoment` fetches the player chunk, the animation and the WebAssembly once, in idle time, when an
  ending is one step away:
  - one card left in the Inbox;
  - one card left in a review round;
  - the Close the day page.

  So the 700 ms window is met on a first web visit too.

**LT8 · the ending felt where it lands** (`LOTTIE_LANDS`: Inbox zero 66, Round complete 72, Day closed 80)
- The player sends its host one `lottieland` event on the frame the ✓ or ∎ lands (or on `complete`, if a frame is
  skipped). `LottieMoment` with `feel` then plays `complete` once.
- Without the animation (reduced motion, Save-Data, a late player), it is felt when the still shows.
- Screens pass `feel` only when the ending just happened:
  - the Inbox, only after its last card was decided on this visit, so an Inbox opened empty is not felt;
  - Round complete;
  - Day closed, after closing.

**LT9 · nothing grows from nothing** (`scripts/lottie/build.mjs`, regenerated)
- Day closed's two stars fade in from 70% of their size.
- A badge first draws its ring (an accent line inside the disc's edge), then its disc fades in from 70% under it, and
  the tick is drawn on the disc while it settles.
- The ✓ and ∎ land on the same frames, and the last frame is still the A2 still. The existing LT4, LT5 and LT6 tests
  pass unchanged.
