## N128 The A2 illustrations, redrawn clean and symmetric (12 Nov 2026)

The Owner found the first A2 set (N127) "too weird" (story scenes: a compass on a notebook, a ruler, a dome sky).
They are redrawn in a plainer house style the Owner approved, then tightened for symmetry:

- one familiar object per state, centred on a soft round backdrop (`--v7-role-surface-muted`), drawn at one line
  weight (3 units) with round caps;
- one accent per drawing (subject colour, else brand indigo): a flag, a waypoint, a star, a clock hand, or a badge;
- badges (✓ or ＋) always sit on the object's bottom-right corner; the Atlas stars balance about the centre.
