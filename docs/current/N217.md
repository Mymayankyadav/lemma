## N217 Scribe replay measurement

This PR adds a development-only replay of the existing N201 Tutor notation engine, using a frozen synthetic fixture and main's actual source. It separates returned allowed candidate recall from visible Top-1, Top-3 and MRR@3, with exact random expectations and pool-size slices. Regressions cover ordering, all 720 permutations of a six-candidate real engine pool, a hidden strip, missing targets and three synthetic learner-choice probes. The [versioned protocol](../research/scribe/replay-v1.md) records bounds, reproduction and results. N202's unmerged adaptive C0, real learner performance, WPM, safety certification and device benchmarks were not measured. No product code, telemetry, dependencies or rollout change.
