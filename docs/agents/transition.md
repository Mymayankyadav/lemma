## Rollback

Authorized controller records rollback for actual affected cohort/scopes, stops new conflicting claims, checkpoints/drains and pauses starts only where authorized. Restore coherent known startup/config/protocol through ordinary reviewed or scoped owner-authorized integration while retaining valid safety fixes, owner amendments, task approvals, holds and lineage. Never blanket-revert app/data history or infer that old fixed-capacity text applies to a larger current workforce. Read back actual affected participants, reconcile current work and live conflicts, and declare restored authority only with sufficient evidence. If legacy cannot represent current membership/work safely, remain contained and prepare a compatible recovery package.

A missing packet with safe fallback is not automatic global rollback. New security/data-loss facts or unresolved authority races justify scoped containment; failure of a timing budget alone is not a blanket freeze after an owner-approved integration. Unknown remote results are reconciled before retries.
