# v6 design mockup captures (the target)

These 19 PNGs are 390×844 captures of the Owner's v6 design mockup, `docs/v6/prototype/mockup.html`, rendered with system fallback fonts (Today, Paths, Review, Calendar, Progress, Inbox, Cards, Atlas, Settings in Day and Night; Focus in Day). They are the visual target the v6 app must match. They are not captures of the app.

Never overwrite or replace them; `tests/unit/docs/mockup-screens.test.ts` fails if any changes. Captures of the real app go in `docs/v6/release-captures/` (see its README).
