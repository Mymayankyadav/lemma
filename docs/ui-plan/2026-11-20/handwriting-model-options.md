# Offline handwriting model options

The first comparison for the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 phone with 6 GB RAM should use the current Draw beta and real finger handwriting. Detypify already works offline with explicit candidate selection. Symbolizer is the closest smaller model to compare if its author grants permission for the weights and labels. A small classifier trained on recorded stroke sequences would be the next build option if important symbols remain hard to distinguish. [Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 product brief](https://www.qualcomm.com/content/dam/qcomm-martech/dm-assets/documents/Snapdragon-4-Gen-4-Product-Brief.pdf)

The project page and public repository do not state a license for the weights or labels, and the reported scores do not identify a comparable writer split or phone benchmark. Obtain permission and pin the model and label order before testing. A smaller model would still need the WASM runtime. [Symbolizer releases and evaluation](https://github.com/zimya/symbolizer#model) |
| **MathNote OCR** | Apache-2.0 project with a 0.65M-parameter raster symbol classifier inside a roughly 1.2M-parameter expression pipeline. | **Google ML Kit Digital Ink** | Native Android and iOS SDK uses vector strokes and runs on-device once its model is downloaded. ## How to compare on the phone

The first synthetic holdout distinguishes identity from completion. For 200 finished traces across 20 model-supported symbols, Detypify put the intended glyph in its first three classes every time. The separate matcher proposed the intended continuation for 53 of 100 partial traces. [First synthetic comparison](../../implementation/2026-11-21/ink-simulation/README.md)

The harder handwriting-style stress test recalled 172/204 completed targets in the displayed strip and 22/66 intended continuations in their extension group. All 168 completed targets with an exact model class were in its top three, while the geometry-only targets accounted for the 32 completed misses. Five candidates did not improve recall in either corpus. An alternate classifier could improve real-writer robustness and coverage of `<`, `>`, `=`, and `≠`, which have no exact Detypify class. Even a perfect classifier of the visible strokes cannot distinguish a finished `<` from the same strokes drawn on the way to `≤`. Keep partial continuation and insertion gates separate from model scoring. These are synthetic rates, not human-writer accuracy. [Human-like stress results](../../implementation/2026-11-21/ink-simulation/humanlike-stress/README.md)

Measure cold readiness, warm time from pen-up until candidates paint, and peak memory in the actual Android WebView. Compare exact top-1, top-3 and top-5 results on the same completed drawings, with per-symbol confusion counts and false suggestions for incomplete strokes, dots and unknown marks. Include fingers, different stroke orders, and different writers. Prioritize \(\forall,\exists,\in,\notin,\subset,\subseteq,<,\le,>,\ge,=,\ne,\infty\) and distinct Greek cases. Keep calibration and evaluation writers separate.

Lemma's existing benchmark is on desktop Chromium. Warm worker p95 was 35.4 ms for the web app and 55.1 ms for the Android-mode bundle in that environment, without the UI's pen-up delay or rendering. These figures do not predict p95 or memory headroom on the 6 GB phone. No alternative was benchmarked on the target phone or added to the app. [Runtime benchmark conditions](../../../public/models/tutor-symbols/detypify-0.3.0-lemma-v1/README.md)

A small stroke-sequence classifier could reuse Lemma's captured x/y and pen-up boundaries, preserve stroke direction and order, and classify a bounded subset of proof symbols. HWRT is a published source of isolated vector strokes with ODbL terms. It includes Detexify data. HASYv2 is a source of rasterized isolated symbols. Either can support a baseline comparison after checking rights and coverage, but candidate training provenance and duplicate traces must be checked before treating their scores as held-out evidence. Fresh consenting writers are the clearest independent evaluation. [HWRT dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/50022) · [HASYv2 dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/259444)
