For touch typing, the owner chose the custom keyboard with a direct **Draw symbol** key. Default suggestions complete started vocabulary, offer exact aliases and reformat fully supplied shorthand. Mathematical templates remain explicit choices with learner-filled placeholders. The new symbol pad handles a single glyph, and the image scratchpad handles full drawings and proofs. N200 equation OCR remains separate.

The replacement visual pack includes menus, editors and scrolled content. The supplied ZIP contained 50 visual assets, including composite evidence. The review uses those assets alongside reconstructed reachable baseline states because the referenced 492 individual originals were unavailable.
