The small print

Privacy, in plain language.

Lemma is a personal mathematics study organiser. Your projects and notes belong to you; the app does not sell them or use them for advertising.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and sign-in information needed for authentication.
  • Study activity: projects, plans, checkpoints, flashcards, review results, sessions, tutor notes, settings and optional reminder preferences.
  • Files: PDFs you upload and their extracted page text, attached to your study projects.

Data is stored through Supabase in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region. Access to study records is restricted to your account.

How it is used

Lemma uses this information to show your study plan, resume progress, schedule card reviews and sync your records across sessions. When you use a connected tutor, relevant study information is shared with that tutor service at your request; that service has its own privacy terms.

There are no ads, no analytics and no sale of personal data. Lemma does not share your study records with advertisers or data brokers. Supabase provides the storage and authentication infrastructure.

Export and deletion

Use Settings → Export to keep a copy of your study information. To request deletion of your account, projects and uploaded PDFs, email the app owner using the developer contact address in the Google Play listing. Do not post personal data in a public issue.

Deletion is handled on request. Some minimal service records may be retained where required for security or legal obligations.

Security and changes

Connections use HTTPS. Account access controls protect private study records; avoid sharing your connector address or sign-in credentials. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
